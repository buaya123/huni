# Content Moderation & Enforcement Policy

## Plain Language Summary

Huni exists to encourage open, respectful, and community-focused discussions.

This Policy explains how Huni reviews reports, investigates potential violations, and enforces its Community Guidelines and other platform policies.

Our moderation philosophy is guided by several core principles:

- We moderate conduct, not viewpoints.
- Anonymous participation does not mean freedom from accountability.
- Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and consistent.
- Context matters.
- Good-faith mistakes should generally be treated differently from intentional abuse.
- Users should have opportunities to understand moderation decisions and, where appropriate, request further review.

Not every report results in enforcement, and not every violation results in the same consequence.

Each situation is reviewed individually based on the available information.

This summary is provided for convenience only. The complete Policy below governs.

---

# 1. Introduction

Huni is committed to fostering a safe, respectful, and open platform where users can participate in meaningful discussions while remaining accountable for their conduct.

This Content Moderation & Enforcement Policy explains how Huni reviews reported content, evaluates potential policy violations, and determines appropriate enforcement actions.

The purpose of moderation is not to control opinions or suppress lawful expression, but to protect the integrity of the platform and the safety of its communities.

---

## 1.1 Scope

This Policy applies to all activity on Huni, including but not limited to:

- posts;
- comments;
- profile information;
- usernames;
- images;
- videos;
- campaign participation;
- Partner activities;
- Scanner activities;
- private reports;
- direct interactions facilitated through the platform; and
- other user-generated content or platform activity.

This Policy applies in conjunction with Huni's:

- Terms of Service;
- Community Guidelines;
- Privacy Policy;
- Rewards Policy;
- Partner Agreement;
- Copyright Policy; and
- other applicable Huni policies.

---

## 1.2 Purpose of Moderation

Moderation exists to:

- promote respectful community participation;
- protect users from harmful conduct;
- encourage honest engagement;
- maintain trust in the platform;
- enforce Huni's published policies fairly;
- protect legitimate community discussions; and
- support a healthy environment for local communities.

Moderation is intended to support the community—not to control it.

---

## 1.3 Moderation Philosophy

Huni's moderation philosophy is built upon the following principles.

### Moderate Conduct, Not Viewpoints

Huni generally moderates behavior rather than opinions.

People may disagree about politics, religion, public policy, businesses, organizations, or other matters without violating Huni's policies.

Moderation focuses on conduct that violates Huni's published rules rather than the viewpoint being expressed.

---

### Anonymous, Never Unaccountable

Huni allows anonymous participation because anonymity can encourage honest discussion.

However, anonymity does not exempt users from complying with Huni's policies or applicable law.

Users remain responsible for their conduct while using the platform.

---

### Fairness

Moderation decisions should be made impartially and in good faith.

Similar situations should generally receive similar treatment while recognizing that no two situations are exactly alike.

---

### Proportionality

Not every violation requires the same response.

Where appropriate, Huni seeks to apply enforcement measures that are proportionate to the seriousness, intent, impact, and frequency of the conduct.

---

### Context Matters

Content should be evaluated in context.

When appropriate, Huni may consider factors including:

- the surrounding discussion;
- the apparent intent of the user;
- whether the conduct appears satirical, educational, journalistic, artistic, or informational;
- prior interactions;
- the potential impact on others; and
- other relevant circumstances.

Content should not be evaluated solely by isolated words or phrases whenever broader context is reasonably available.

---

### Transparency

Where reasonably practicable, Huni seeks to explain significant moderation decisions and provide users with appropriate information regarding enforcement actions affecting their accounts.

Transparency helps build trust in the moderation process.

---

### Continuous Improvement

Online communities evolve over time.

Huni will continue improving its moderation practices through experience, community feedback, technological improvements, and ongoing review of this Policy.

# 2. Moderation Principles

Huni's moderation decisions are guided by principles intended to promote fairness, consistency, accountability, and respect for lawful expression.

These principles apply whenever Huni reviews reported content, investigates potential policy violations, or determines appropriate enforcement actions.

No single principle automatically overrides another. Moderation decisions should balance these principles based on the specific circumstances of each case.

---

## 2.1 Impartiality

Moderation decisions should be made impartially.

Users should not receive favorable or unfavorable treatment based solely on their:

- political beliefs;
- religion;
- nationality;
- ethnicity;
- gender;
- profession;
- organization;
- popularity;
- personal relationships; or
- other unrelated characteristics.

Moderation focuses on conduct and compliance with Huni's published policies.

---

## 2.2 Evidence-Based Review

Moderation decisions should be based on reasonably available information.

Where appropriate, moderators may consider:

- reported content;
- surrounding conversations;
- user reports;
- platform records;
- applicable policies;
- prior moderation history; and
- other relevant evidence.

Speculation or assumptions alone should not determine moderation outcomes.

---

## 2.3 Contextual Evaluation

Content should be evaluated within its overall context.

Moderators may consider factors including:

- the surrounding discussion;
- whether statements appear serious or satirical;
- educational or journalistic purposes;
- artistic expression;
- cultural context;
- the apparent intent of the user; and
- the likely interpretation by a reasonable reader.

Context helps distinguish genuine policy violations from lawful expression.

---

## 2.4 Consistency

Similar conduct should generally receive similar moderation outcomes.

While no two situations are identical, Huni strives to apply its policies consistently across users and communities.

Consistency promotes trust and predictability within the platform.

---

## 2.5 Proportionality

Moderation responses should be proportionate to the circumstances.

Factors that may be considered include:

- seriousness of the conduct;
- actual or potential impact;
- intent;
- frequency;
- prior violations;
- cooperation during review; and
- other relevant circumstances.

Whenever reasonably appropriate, Huni may choose the least restrictive enforcement measure that effectively addresses the violation.

---

## 2.6 Timeliness

Huni will make reasonable efforts to review reports and moderation matters in a timely manner.

The time required for review may vary depending on:

- the complexity of the matter;
- available evidence;
- platform activity;
- potential risks to users;
- legal considerations; or
- other operational factors.

Urgent matters affecting user safety or platform security may receive priority review.

---

## 2.7 Good-Faith Participation

Huni recognizes that users sometimes make honest mistakes.

Where appropriate, moderators may distinguish between:

- intentional misconduct;
- negligent conduct;
- misunderstandings;
- accidental violations; and
- good-faith participation.

Good-faith mistakes may warrant a different response than deliberate abuse.

---

## 2.8 Protection Against Abuse

Moderation tools must not be used to:

- suppress lawful opinions;
- retaliate against users;
- favor particular viewpoints;
- gain personal advantage;
- interfere with legitimate community participation; or
- otherwise misuse Huni's moderation processes.

Huni expects moderators and users alike to participate honestly and responsibly.

---

## 2.9 Accountability

Moderation decisions are subject to Huni's internal review processes.

Where appropriate, significant moderation actions may be reviewed to promote consistency, identify errors, improve moderation practices, and strengthen community trust.

Moderators are expected to apply Huni's policies responsibly and in good faith.

---

## 2.10 Continuous Learning

Moderation is an evolving responsibility.

Huni may improve its moderation practices by:

- reviewing moderation outcomes;
- considering community feedback;
- improving moderator guidance;
- adopting new moderation technologies;
- refining internal procedures; and
- updating this Policy as the platform evolves.

Continuous improvement helps ensure that moderation remains fair, effective, and responsive to the needs of the Huni community.

# 3. Community Reports

Huni encourages users to report content or conduct that they reasonably believe violates the Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, or other Huni policies.

Community reports help identify potential issues that may not otherwise come to Huni's attention.

Submitting a report begins an administrative review process. It does not automatically result in enforcement action.

---

## 3.1 Who May Submit Reports

Reports may be submitted by:

- users;
- Partners;
- authorized Scanners;
- affected individuals;
- organizations where appropriate; or
- other persons with a legitimate interest in the reported conduct.

Reports should relate to conduct occurring on or through the Huni platform unless another Huni policy expressly provides otherwise.

---

## 3.2 What May Be Reported

Users are encouraged to report conduct they reasonably believe violates Huni's policies.

Examples may include:

- harassment or abusive behavior;
- threats of violence;
- hate speech prohibited by the Community Guidelines;
- spam or deceptive activity;
- impersonation;
- fraud or scams;
- unlawful content;
- privacy violations;
- misuse of campaigns or rewards;
- abuse of Partner or Scanner privileges; or
- other conduct prohibited by Huni's published policies.

Disagreement with another user's opinion, by itself, is generally not a basis for moderation.

---

## 3.3 Information to Include

To assist Huni in reviewing a report, users should provide, where reasonably available:

- the reported content or activity;
- the reason for the report;
- relevant context;
- supporting screenshots or other evidence, where available;
- the approximate date and time of the incident; and
- any additional information that may assist the review.

Providing complete and accurate information helps Huni evaluate reports more effectively.

---

## 3.4 Good-Faith Reporting

Reports should be submitted only where the reporting party reasonably believes that a policy violation may have occurred.

Users should not submit reports:

- to harass another person;
- to retaliate against someone for expressing a lawful opinion;
- to interfere with legitimate community participation;
- to gain a personal or competitive advantage; or
- for other improper purposes.

Knowingly submitting false or malicious reports may itself violate Huni's policies.

---

## 3.5 Review Priority

Huni may prioritize reports based on factors including:

- potential risk to user safety;
- suspected unlawful activity;
- ongoing or widespread harm;
- platform security concerns;
- abuse affecting multiple users;
- potential impact on the community; or
- other operational considerations.

Reports are not necessarily reviewed in the order they are received.

---

## 3.6 Multiple Reports

Multiple reports concerning the same content do not automatically indicate that a policy violation has occurred.

Similarly, the absence of reports does not prevent Huni from reviewing content where it otherwise becomes aware of a potential violation.

Moderation decisions are based on the available evidence and applicable policies, not solely on the number of reports received.

---

## 3.7 Additional Information

During the review process, Huni may request additional information from:

- the reporting party;
- the reported user;
- affected individuals;
- Partners;
- authorized Scanners; or
- other persons reasonably able to assist in evaluating the matter.

Failure to provide requested information may affect Huni's ability to complete its review.

---

## 3.8 Confidentiality of Reports

Huni will handle reports in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable law.

To protect the integrity of the moderation process, Huni generally does not disclose the identity of a reporting party to the reported user unless required by law or reasonably necessary to resolve the matter.

---

## 3.9 No Guaranteed Outcome

Submitting a report does not guarantee that:

- content will be removed;
- enforcement action will be taken;
- an account will be restricted;
- Huni will agree with the reporting party's assessment; or
- the reporting party will receive a detailed explanation of the final decision.

Each report is evaluated individually based on the available information and Huni's published policies.

---

## 3.10 Community Responsibility

A healthy community depends on responsible participation.

Huni encourages users to report genuine policy violations while respecting the rights of others to engage in lawful discussion, express differing opinions, and participate in the platform without fear of retaliation or misuse of the reporting system.

# 4. Moderation Review Process

When Huni receives a report or otherwise becomes aware of a potential policy violation, Huni may review the matter to determine whether action is appropriate.

Moderation reviews are administrative processes intended to evaluate reported conduct fairly, consistently, and in accordance with Huni's published policies.

No report is presumed to be valid or invalid solely because it has been submitted.

---

## 4.1 Initial Assessment

Upon receiving a report, Huni may conduct an initial assessment to determine:

- whether sufficient information has been provided;
- whether the reported matter appears to fall within Huni's policies;
- whether immediate action may be necessary to protect users or the platform; and
- whether additional review is appropriate.

Reports that are clearly incomplete, duplicate, or unrelated to Huni's policies may receive limited review or be closed without further action.

---

## 4.2 Collection of Information

Where appropriate, Huni may gather additional information relevant to the reported matter.

This may include:

- the reported content;
- surrounding conversations;
- related posts or comments;
- platform activity records;
- prior moderation history;
- reports submitted by other users;
- technical information reasonably necessary to understand the incident; and
- other relevant evidence.

Only information reasonably necessary for the review will be considered.

---

## 4.3 Contextual Review

Moderators should evaluate reported conduct in its full context whenever reasonably possible.

Context may include:

- the surrounding discussion;
- the apparent intent of the user;
- previous interactions between the parties;
- cultural or local context;
- whether the content appears educational, journalistic, artistic, satirical, or informational;
- the likely understanding of a reasonable reader; and
- other relevant circumstances.

Content should not ordinarily be evaluated based solely on isolated words, images, or excerpts.

---

## 4.4 Policy Evaluation

After reviewing the available information, Huni will evaluate whether the reported conduct appears to violate:

- the Community Guidelines;
- the Terms of Service;
- this Policy;
- the Copyright Policy;
- the Rewards Policy;
- the Partner Agreement; or
- other applicable Huni policies.

Moderators should apply the policies consistently and in accordance with the Moderation Principles described in this Policy.

---

## 4.5 Additional Information

Where reasonably necessary, Huni may request additional information from:

- the reporting party;
- the reported user;
- affected users;
- Partners;
- authorized Scanners; or
- other persons reasonably able to assist in evaluating the matter.

Failure to provide requested information may affect Huni's ability to complete the review.

---

## 4.6 Conflicts of Interest

Moderation decisions should be made as impartially as reasonably possible.

Where a moderator has a personal, financial, professional, or other significant conflict of interest relating to a reported matter, Huni should take reasonable steps to ensure that another qualified moderator or reviewer handles the matter where practicable.

---

## 4.7 Administrative Decision

Following review, Huni may determine that:

- no policy violation occurred;
- additional monitoring is appropriate;
- educational guidance is sufficient;
- corrective action is appropriate;
- enforcement action is warranted; or
- no further action is necessary.

Each decision should be based on the available information and applicable policies.

---

## 4.8 Urgent Matters

Certain situations may require expedited review.

Examples include:

- credible threats of violence;
- significant risks to user safety;
- suspected child exploitation;
- ongoing fraud or scams;
- major platform security concerns;
- unlawful content requiring prompt attention; or
- other circumstances presenting an immediate and significant risk.

Urgent matters may receive priority handling over routine moderation reports.

---

## 4.9 Recordkeeping

Huni may maintain internal records relating to moderation reviews, including:

- reports received;
- information reviewed;
- moderation decisions;
- enforcement actions;
- appeals; and
- other administrative records.

Such records help promote consistency, improve moderation practices, resolve future disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Records will be handled in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

---

## 4.10 Good-Faith Administration

Moderation reviews are conducted in good faith based on the information reasonably available at the time.

Administrative decisions made under this Policy are intended to support the safe and fair operation of the Huni platform and do not constitute judicial or legal determinations of the rights or liabilities of any party.

# 5. Enforcement Actions

Where Huni reasonably determines that a user has violated its policies, Huni may take appropriate enforcement action.

Enforcement actions are intended to:

- protect the Huni community;
- encourage compliance with Huni's policies;
- prevent continued or repeated violations;
- maintain the integrity of the platform; and
- support fair and consistent moderation.

The specific enforcement action taken will depend on the circumstances of each case.

---

## 5.1 Factors Considered

When determining an appropriate enforcement action, Huni may consider factors including:

- the seriousness of the violation;
- the actual or potential impact on others;
- whether the conduct appears intentional or accidental;
- the user's prior moderation history;
- cooperation during the review process;
- efforts to correct the violation;
- whether the violation is ongoing; and
- other relevant circumstances.

No single factor automatically determines the outcome.

---

## 5.2 Educational Guidance

Where appropriate, Huni may respond to minor or first-time violations by providing educational guidance.

Educational guidance may include:

- explaining the applicable policy;
- encouraging corrective action;
- directing users to relevant Huni resources; or
- reminding users of community expectations.

Educational guidance is intended to promote understanding rather than punishment.

---

## 5.3 Warnings

Huni may issue a warning where a violation has occurred but stronger enforcement is not yet considered necessary.

Warnings may inform users of:

- the policy involved;
- the conduct giving rise to the warning;
- possible future consequences of repeated violations; and
- available resources for understanding Huni's policies.

A warning does not necessarily restrict a user's account.

---

## 5.4 Content Actions

Where appropriate, Huni may take action relating to specific content, including:

- applying informational labels;
- limiting the visibility of content;
- restricting sharing;
- removing content;
- disabling access to content; or
- taking other reasonable measures relating to the content itself.

Content actions do not necessarily affect the user's account beyond the specific content involved.

---

## 5.5 Feature Restrictions

Where appropriate, Huni may temporarily restrict access to certain platform features.

Examples include restrictions on:

- posting;
- commenting;
- creating campaigns;
- managing Partner activities;
- Scanner privileges;
- reward participation; or
- other platform features.

Restrictions may be temporary or indefinite depending on the circumstances.

---

## 5.6 Account Suspension

Where a violation is more serious or repeated, Huni may temporarily suspend a user's account.

During a suspension, users may lose access to some or all platform features.

The duration of a suspension may vary depending on the circumstances of the violation.

---

## 5.7 Permanent Account Termination

Huni may permanently terminate an account where reasonably necessary, including where:

- repeated serious violations occur;
- the user engages in significant fraud or abuse;
- unlawful conduct substantially affects the platform;
- platform security is intentionally compromised;
- the user repeatedly circumvents enforcement measures; or
- continued participation presents a significant risk to the Huni community or platform.

Permanent termination is generally reserved for the most serious situations.

---

## 5.8 Immediate Enforcement

Certain conduct may justify immediate enforcement without prior warning.

Examples may include:

- credible threats of violence;
- child exploitation;
- significant fraud;
- coordinated abuse of the platform;
- serious security attacks;
- unlawful activity presenting an immediate risk; or
- other exceptionally serious violations.

Immediate enforcement will generally be limited to situations where prompt action is reasonably necessary.

---

## 5.9 Combined Enforcement

Where appropriate, Huni may apply more than one enforcement measure at the same time.

For example, Huni may:

- remove violating content while issuing a warning;
- suspend an account while restricting specific platform features; or
- terminate an account following repeated or particularly serious violations.

Combined enforcement should remain proportionate to the circumstances.

---

## 5.10 Purpose of Enforcement

Enforcement is intended to promote a safe, respectful, and trustworthy community.

Whenever reasonably appropriate, Huni seeks to encourage future compliance rather than impose punishment for its own sake.

The objective of moderation is to improve the health of the community while respecting lawful participation and the rights of all users.

# 6. Temporary Restrictions

In certain circumstances, Huni may temporarily restrict access to content, platform features, or user accounts.

Temporary restrictions are administrative measures intended to protect users, preserve the integrity of the platform, and encourage compliance with Huni's policies.

Temporary restrictions are generally not intended to permanently exclude users from the Huni community.

---

## 6.1 Purpose

Temporary restrictions may be used to:

- prevent ongoing policy violations;
- reduce the risk of harm to users;
- protect the integrity of investigations;
- encourage corrective action;
- preserve platform security; or
- maintain community trust while a matter is being resolved.

Whenever reasonably appropriate, temporary restrictions should be proportionate to the circumstances.

---

## 6.2 Types of Temporary Restrictions

Depending on the circumstances, Huni may temporarily:

- remove or restrict access to content;
- limit content visibility;
- disable posting;
- disable commenting;
- restrict messaging features;
- suspend campaign participation;
- suspend Partner privileges;
- suspend Scanner privileges;
- restrict reward-related features;
- temporarily suspend an account; or
- apply other reasonable administrative measures.

The type of restriction imposed should correspond to the nature of the policy concern.

---

## 6.3 Duration

The duration of a temporary restriction may vary depending on:

- the seriousness of the conduct;
- the purpose of the restriction;
- whether additional review is required;
- cooperation during the review process;
- repeated violations; or
- other relevant circumstances.

Temporary restrictions may end automatically, be lifted following review, or remain in effect until specified conditions have been satisfied.

---

## 6.4 User Responsibilities

While a temporary restriction is in effect, users are expected to:

- comply with the restriction;
- refrain from attempting to bypass enforcement;
- cooperate with reasonable requests for information;
- avoid repeating the conduct that gave rise to the restriction; and
- continue complying with Huni's policies.

Compliance during a temporary restriction may be considered in future moderation decisions where appropriate.

---

## 6.5 Circumvention

Users must not attempt to avoid temporary restrictions by:

- creating additional accounts;
- using another person's account;
- asking another person to act on their behalf to evade the restriction;
- exploiting technical vulnerabilities;
- repeatedly reposting removed content; or
- using other methods intended to defeat enforcement.

Attempts to circumvent temporary restrictions may result in additional enforcement action.

---

## 6.6 Review During Restriction

Where appropriate, Huni may continue reviewing the matter while a temporary restriction is in effect.

During this period, Huni may:

- gather additional information;
- review new evidence;
- consult applicable policies;
- modify the restriction;
- remove the restriction; or
- impose additional enforcement where reasonably justified.

Temporary restrictions may therefore change as additional information becomes available.

---

## 6.7 Early Removal

Where appropriate, Huni may lift a temporary restriction before its anticipated conclusion if:

- the underlying concern has been resolved;
- additional information changes the outcome;
- the restriction is no longer necessary;
- the restriction was imposed in error; or
- other circumstances reasonably justify early removal.

Early removal does not necessarily prevent future moderation if new information becomes available.

---

## 6.8 Effect on Platform Activity

Temporary restrictions may limit a user's ability to participate in some platform features while leaving other features available.

For example, a user may be prevented from creating new posts while still being able to:

- read public content;
- manage account settings;
- review moderation notices;
- submit an appeal where available; or
- access other unaffected features.

The specific limitations depend on the enforcement action applied.

---

## 6.9 No Permanent Determination

The existence of a temporary restriction does not necessarily indicate that permanent enforcement will follow.

Temporary restrictions are administrative measures intended to address immediate concerns while allowing appropriate review and consideration of the available information.

---

## 6.10 Return to Normal Participation

Where a temporary restriction expires or is lifted, users are encouraged to resume participation in accordance with Huni's policies.

The purpose of temporary restrictions is to support future compliance and healthy community participation rather than permanently discourage engagement.

# 7. Permanent Enforcement

In exceptional circumstances, Huni may permanently remove content, permanently revoke platform privileges, or permanently terminate user accounts.

Permanent enforcement is reserved for situations where lesser enforcement measures are insufficient to protect the Huni community, maintain platform integrity, or comply with applicable law.

Whenever reasonably practicable, Huni seeks to apply permanent enforcement fairly, consistently, and proportionately.

---

## 7.1 Purpose

Permanent enforcement exists to:

- protect users from serious or ongoing harm;
- preserve trust in the platform;
- prevent repeated or intentional abuse;
- protect the integrity of Huni's services;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- support the long-term health of the Huni community.

Permanent enforcement is not intended to be punitive for its own sake.

---

## 7.2 Permanent Content Removal

Huni may permanently remove content where it reasonably determines that:

- the content seriously violates Huni's policies;
- restoration would create an ongoing risk to users or the platform;
- removal is required by applicable law;
- removal is necessary to protect the rights of others; or
- other circumstances reasonably justify permanent removal.

Removal of content does not necessarily result in permanent action against the user's account.

---

## 7.3 Permanent Revocation of Privileges

Where appropriate, Huni may permanently revoke specific platform privileges without terminating the user's account.

Examples include:

- Partner privileges;
- Scanner privileges;
- campaign creation privileges;
- reward participation privileges; or
- other platform-specific privileges.

Permanent revocation of a privilege does not automatically prevent a user from continuing to participate in other areas of the platform.

---

## 7.4 Permanent Account Termination

Huni may permanently terminate a user's account where reasonably necessary, including where:

- repeated serious policy violations occur;
- the user engages in significant fraud or deception;
- the user repeatedly circumvents enforcement measures;
- the user intentionally compromises platform security;
- unlawful conduct presents a substantial risk to users or the platform;
- child exploitation or similarly egregious conduct is identified; or
- continued participation would present a significant and ongoing risk to the Huni community.

Permanent account termination is generally reserved for the most serious or repeated violations.

---

## 7.5 Factors Considered

Before imposing permanent enforcement, Huni may consider factors including:

- the seriousness of the conduct;
- the actual or potential impact on others;
- whether the conduct appears intentional;
- the user's moderation history;
- previous enforcement actions;
- efforts to correct or discontinue the conduct;
- cooperation during the review process; and
- other relevant circumstances.

Not every serious violation will necessarily result in permanent enforcement.

---

## 7.6 Effect of Permanent Enforcement

Depending on the enforcement action applied, a user may permanently lose access to:

- specific content;
- platform features;
- Partner privileges;
- Scanner privileges;
- reward-related features;
- campaign management tools; or
- the Huni account itself.

The specific consequences depend on the nature of the enforcement action.

---

## 7.7 Circumvention

Users whose accounts or privileges have been permanently restricted must not attempt to circumvent enforcement by:

- creating additional accounts;
- using another person's account;
- creating replacement Partner accounts;
- obtaining Scanner privileges through another user;
- using automated methods to regain access; or
- otherwise attempting to evade Huni's enforcement measures.

Attempts to circumvent permanent enforcement may result in additional administrative or legal action where appropriate.

---

## 7.8 Appeals

Where permitted under this Policy, users subject to permanent enforcement may request a review through Huni's appeals process.

Submission of an appeal does not automatically suspend or reverse the enforcement action.

Appeals are reviewed in accordance with Article 8 of this Policy.

---

## 7.9 Administrative Finality

Following completion of Huni's administrative review and any applicable appeal process, Huni's enforcement decision will generally be considered final for purposes of operating the platform.

Nothing in this Article limits any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

---

## 7.10 Protection of the Community

Permanent enforcement is intended to protect the Huni community and preserve confidence in the platform.

While Huni seeks to encourage rehabilitation and future compliance wherever reasonably possible, protecting the safety, integrity, and trust of the community remains the primary consideration when permanent enforcement becomes necessary.

# 8. Appeals

Huni recognizes that moderation decisions may occasionally be incomplete, based on limited information, or made in error.

Where permitted under this Policy, users may request an administrative review of certain moderation decisions through Huni's appeals process.

The purpose of an appeal is to determine whether the original decision remains appropriate based on the available information and Huni's published policies.

---

## 8.1 Right to Request Review

Subject to this Policy, users may request review of eligible moderation decisions affecting:

- removed content;
- feature restrictions;
- temporary suspensions;
- permanent revocation of privileges;
- permanent account termination; or
- other significant enforcement actions identified by Huni.

Not every moderation action is necessarily eligible for appeal.

---

## 8.2 Appeal Period

Appeals should generally be submitted within the period specified by Huni for the applicable enforcement action.

Appeals submitted after the applicable period may be declined unless Huni reasonably determines that exceptional circumstances justify a late review.

---

## 8.3 Information to Include

To assist in reviewing an appeal, users should provide, where reasonably available:

- the moderation decision being appealed;
- the reason the user believes the decision should be reconsidered;
- any additional facts or context;
- supporting evidence, where available; and
- any other information that may assist the review.

Repeating the original disagreement without additional explanation may not assist the appeal process.

---

## 8.4 Scope of Review

An appeal is a review of Huni's administrative decision.

During the review, Huni may consider:

- the original report;
- the evidence previously reviewed;
- additional information submitted during the appeal;
- applicable Huni policies;
- subsequent developments relevant to the matter; and
- other information reasonably necessary to evaluate the decision.

The appeal is not limited solely to the arguments presented by either party.

---

## 8.5 Independent Review

Where reasonably practicable, appeals should be reviewed by a moderator or reviewer who was not primarily responsible for the original decision.

If this is not reasonably practicable, Huni will make reasonable efforts to ensure that the appeal is reviewed fairly and impartially.

---

## 8.6 Possible Outcomes

Following review, Huni may:

- affirm the original decision;
- modify the enforcement action;
- reduce or remove restrictions;
- restore content;
- restore platform privileges;
- reverse the decision entirely; or
- take other reasonable administrative action.

Each appeal will be decided based on the available information and applicable policies.

---

## 8.7 Additional Information

Where reasonably necessary, Huni may request additional information from:

- the appealing user;
- affected individuals;
- reporting parties, where appropriate;
- Partners;
- authorized Scanners; or
- other persons reasonably able to assist the review.

Failure to provide requested information may affect Huni's ability to complete the appeal.

---

## 8.8 Effect During Appeal

Unless Huni determines otherwise, submitting an appeal does not automatically suspend or reverse the enforcement action under review.

The original moderation decision generally remains in effect while the appeal is pending.

---

## 8.9 Final Administrative Decision

After completing the appeal, Huni will issue a final administrative decision for purposes of operating the platform.

While users remain free to exercise any rights available under applicable law, Huni is generally not required to conduct repeated reviews of the same matter unless significant new information becomes available.

---

## 8.10 Commitment to Fairness

Appeals help improve the quality, consistency, and accountability of Huni's moderation system.

Huni welcomes good-faith appeals and recognizes that correcting mistakes strengthens community trust.

The availability of an appeal should not be interpreted as evidence that the original decision was improper, nor should the denial of an appeal be interpreted as a judgment beyond the operation of the Huni platform.

# 9. Transparency & Communication

Huni recognizes that transparency promotes trust, accountability, and confidence in its moderation processes.

Where reasonably appropriate, Huni will communicate moderation decisions in a manner that helps users understand the outcome while protecting privacy, platform security, and the integrity of moderation operations.

Transparency is balanced against the need to prevent abuse of Huni's systems and to protect users and moderators.

---

## 9.1 Moderation Notices

Where reasonably practicable, Huni may notify affected users when significant moderation action is taken against:

- their content;
- their account;
- their platform privileges; or
- other aspects of their participation.

Notices may describe the general reason for the action and, where appropriate, identify the relevant Huni policy.

---

## 9.2 Information Provided

Moderation notices may include information such as:

- the type of enforcement action;
- the general policy involved;
- whether the action is temporary or permanent;
- the effective date of the action;
- available appeal rights, where applicable; and
- other information Huni reasonably determines appropriate.

Huni is not required to disclose every detail considered during its review.

---

## 9.3 Protection of Privacy

To protect users and maintain confidence in the moderation process, Huni generally will not disclose:

- the identity of reporting parties;
- confidential personal information;
- internal moderator communications;
- investigative methods;
- platform security measures;
- information protected by law; or
- other confidential operational information.

These limitations help protect the safety, privacy, and integrity of the platform.

---

## 9.4 Protection Against System Abuse

Huni may withhold specific operational details where disclosure could reasonably:

- facilitate circumvention of moderation measures;
- assist fraudulent activity;
- compromise platform security;
- expose vulnerabilities;
- interfere with ongoing reviews; or
- otherwise undermine the effective operation of Huni's moderation systems.

Transparency should not create opportunities for abuse.

---

## 9.5 Public Guidance

Huni may publish educational materials, policy updates, moderation guidance, frequently asked questions, or similar resources to help users better understand community expectations.

Such materials are intended to promote voluntary compliance and informed participation.

---

## 9.6 Policy Updates

Huni may update this Policy from time to time to reflect:

- changes in platform features;
- operational experience;
- legal developments;
- community feedback;
- improvements in moderation practices; or
- other relevant circumstances.

Material changes may be communicated through reasonable platform notices or other appropriate methods.

---

## 9.7 Transparency Reporting

From time to time, Huni may publish aggregated information relating to moderation activities, including:

- the number of reports received;
- categories of reported conduct;
- enforcement actions taken;
- appeal outcomes;
- policy updates; or
- other statistical information.

Any published information will generally be presented in aggregate form and should not identify individual users unless required by law.

---

## 9.8 Community Feedback

Huni values constructive feedback regarding its moderation policies and practices.

Community feedback may be considered when evaluating:

- moderation procedures;
- policy clarity;
- educational resources;
- platform features; or
- future policy improvements.

Providing feedback does not guarantee that a particular suggestion will be adopted.

---

## 9.9 Communication Limitations

While Huni strives to communicate moderation decisions clearly, it may limit or decline additional explanations where reasonably necessary to:

- protect user privacy;
- safeguard moderators;
- preserve investigative integrity;
- prevent repeated abuse of moderation processes;
- comply with legal obligations; or
- protect platform security.

The absence of additional detail should not be interpreted as evidence that a review was incomplete or improper.

---

## 9.10 Commitment to Accountability

Transparency is an ongoing commitment rather than a single action.

Huni seeks to communicate moderation decisions honestly, improve its policies over time, and remain accountable to the community while protecting the rights, privacy, and safety of all users.

Responsible transparency strengthens trust without compromising the effective operation of the platform.

# 10. Cooperation with Legal Authorities

Huni respects the privacy and anonymity of its users while recognizing that it may have legal obligations under applicable law.

Where required or permitted by law, Huni may cooperate with courts, law enforcement agencies, regulatory authorities, or other government bodies in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Such cooperation is intended to support the lawful administration of justice while protecting user rights to the greatest extent reasonably possible.

---

## 10.1 General Principle

Huni is committed to protecting user privacy and anonymous participation.

Except where required or permitted by applicable law, Huni will not disclose user information to third parties solely because content is controversial, unpopular, or the subject of disagreement.

Lawful expression remains protected under Huni's policies regardless of differing viewpoints.

---

## 10.2 Lawful Requests

Huni may respond to lawful requests from competent authorities where required or permitted by applicable law.

Examples may include requests relating to:

- court orders;
- judicial warrants;
- lawful subpoenas;
- authorized law enforcement investigations;
- regulatory requirements; or
- other legally valid government requests.

Huni may evaluate requests to ensure they appear to have an appropriate legal basis before responding.

---

## 10.3 Emergency Situations

Where Huni reasonably believes that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent death, serious bodily harm, or other significant emergency, Huni may disclose information as permitted or required by applicable law.

Emergency disclosures will be limited to information reasonably necessary under the circumstances.

---

## 10.4 Preservation of Information

Where reasonably necessary or legally required, Huni may preserve relevant records relating to:

- user accounts;
- moderation records;
- platform activity;
- reports;
- appeals;
- technical logs; or
- other information relevant to legal obligations or ongoing investigations.

Preservation of information does not necessarily indicate wrongdoing by any user.

---

## 10.5 User Notification

Where reasonably practicable and not prohibited by law, court order, or legitimate investigative requirements, Huni may notify affected users that their information has been requested or disclosed.

Notification may be delayed or withheld where disclosure would:

- violate applicable law;
- interfere with an investigation;
- endanger any person;
- compromise platform security; or
- otherwise be inappropriate under the circumstances.

---

## 10.6 Scope of Disclosure

Where disclosure is required or permitted by law, Huni will seek to limit the information disclosed to that which is reasonably necessary for the applicable legal purpose.

Huni does not voluntarily disclose more information than is reasonably required under the circumstances.

---

## 10.7 Moderation and Legal Proceedings

Moderation decisions made under this Policy are administrative decisions relating to the operation of the Huni platform.

Such decisions do not constitute legal findings, criminal determinations, or judicial conclusions regarding any user's conduct.

Likewise, legal proceedings do not automatically determine Huni's moderation decisions unless required by applicable law or otherwise deemed appropriate under Huni's policies.

---

## 10.8 International Requests

Where Huni receives requests from authorities outside the jurisdiction governing its operations, Huni may evaluate such requests in accordance with applicable law, relevant legal processes, and its obligations to protect user rights.

Huni may decline requests that do not appear to have an appropriate legal basis.

---

## 10.9 Commitment to Privacy

Huni seeks to balance:

- user privacy;
- anonymous participation;
- legal compliance;
- public safety;
- platform integrity; and
- the rights of affected individuals.

These objectives are considered together when responding to legal requests.

---

## 10.10 Anonymous, Never Unaccountable

Anonymous participation is a defining feature of Huni.

However, anonymity within the platform does not exempt users from lawful accountability.

Where required by applicable law and supported by appropriate legal authority, Huni may comply with lawful requests concerning user information while continuing to protect the privacy and rights of its community to the greatest extent reasonably possible.

Huni's commitment is therefore both to anonymous participation and to responsible accountability under the law.
