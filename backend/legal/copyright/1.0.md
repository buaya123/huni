# Copyright Policy

## Plain Language Summary

Huni respects the intellectual property rights of creators, users, and organizations.

This Copyright Policy explains how copyright concerns are handled on the Huni platform, including how copyright owners may report alleged infringement and how users may respond if they believe their content was removed in error.

### In summary:

- You generally own the content you create and post on Huni.
- Do not post content that you do not have the right to use.
- Copyright owners may submit reports of alleged infringement.
- Huni may remove or restrict access to content while a report is reviewed.
- Users may submit a response if they believe a removal was made in error.
- Repeated copyright infringement may result in account restrictions or termination.

This summary is provided for convenience only. The complete Copyright Policy below governs.

- any other applicable Huni policies.

# 3. Respecting Copyright

Huni encourages users to create and share original content while respecting the intellectual property rights of others.

Users should publish only content they have the legal right to use, reproduce, distribute, or otherwise make available through the Huni platform.

Respecting copyright helps protect creators, promotes innovation, and supports a trustworthy community.

---

## 3.1 Original Content

Users are encouraged to share content they have personally created.

Original content generally presents the lowest risk of copyright infringement and contributes positively to the Huni community.

---

## 3.2 Authorized Use

Users may share content created by others only where they have a lawful basis to do so.

Examples may include:

- permission from the copyright owner;
- a valid license;
- content released under an open license;
- public domain material;
- uses permitted under applicable copyright law; or
- other lawful authorization.

Users are responsible for ensuring that any authorization remains valid.

---

## 3.3 Copyright Infringement

Copyright infringement generally occurs when copyrighted material is used, reproduced, distributed, publicly displayed, or otherwise made available without the permission of the copyright owner or another lawful basis.

Whether particular use constitutes infringement depends on the specific facts, applicable law, and any available rights or exceptions.

---

## 3.4 Lawful Uses

Not every use of copyrighted material is unlawful.

Applicable law may recognize circumstances where copyrighted works may be used without the copyright owner's permission.

The availability and scope of such rights vary depending on the applicable law and the particular circumstances.

Users relying on such rights remain responsible for ensuring their use is lawful.

---

## 3.5 Attribution

Providing credit to the original creator is encouraged where appropriate.

However, attribution alone does not grant permission to use copyrighted material and does not prevent copyright infringement where permission or another lawful basis is required.

---

## 3.6 Repeat Posting of Infringing Content

Users should not repeatedly upload, repost, or distribute content that has previously been removed for copyright infringement.

Repeated infringement may result in additional enforcement action under this Policy and Huni's Terms of Service.

---

## 3.7 Huni's Role

Huni provides a platform for users to share content but does not routinely review all user submissions before publication.

When Huni becomes aware of credible allegations of copyright infringement, it may review the reported content and take appropriate action consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

Huni does not determine copyright ownership except as reasonably necessary to administer reports under this Policy.

---

## 3.8 Respect for Creators

Huni encourages users to respect the work of artists, writers, photographers, musicians, developers, educators, businesses, and other creators.

Supporting original work strengthens both the creative community and the quality of content shared on Huni.

Users who are uncertain whether they may lawfully use particular material should seek permission from the copyright owner or refrain from publishing the content until the necessary rights have been obtained.

# 4. Reporting Copyright Infringement

Huni respects the rights of copyright owners and provides a process for reporting alleged copyright infringement.

If you believe that content available through Huni infringes your copyright or the copyright of a person or organization you are authorized to represent, you may submit a copyright report for review.

Submitting a report does not automatically result in the removal of content.

---

## 4.1 Who May Submit a Report

A copyright report may be submitted by:

- the copyright owner;
- a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner;
- an exclusive licensee where permitted by applicable law; or
- another person with legal authority to assert the relevant copyright.

Huni may request additional information where authority is unclear.

---

## 4.2 Information to Include

To assist Huni in reviewing a report, the reporting party should provide, where reasonably available:

- their name and contact information;
- identification of the copyrighted work;
- identification or location of the allegedly infringing content;
- an explanation of why the content is believed to infringe copyright;
- information demonstrating ownership or authority to act on behalf of the copyright owner; and
- any other information reasonably necessary to evaluate the report.

Incomplete reports may require additional information before review can continue.

---

## 4.3 Good-Faith Reporting

Copyright reports should be submitted only where the reporting party reasonably believes that copyright infringement has occurred.

Submitting knowingly false, misleading, or abusive copyright reports may itself violate this Policy and Huni's Terms of Service.

---

## 4.4 Review of Reports

Upon receiving a copyright report, Huni may:

- review the information provided;
- request additional information from the reporting party;
- request information from the user who posted the content;
- temporarily restrict access to the reported content where appropriate;
- decline to take action where the report appears unsupported; or
- take other reasonable steps necessary to investigate the matter.

Huni will review reports in good faith but does not guarantee any particular outcome.

---

## 4.5 Temporary Measures

Where appropriate, Huni may temporarily:

- remove content from public view;
- disable access to the reported material;
- limit sharing of the content; or
- apply other reasonable measures while a report is reviewed.

Temporary measures are administrative actions and do not constitute a final determination that copyright infringement has occurred.

---

## 4.6 Additional Information

During the review process, Huni may request additional information from either the reporting party or the user who posted the content.

Failure to provide reasonably requested information may affect Huni's ability to evaluate the report.

---

## 4.7 No Legal Determination

Huni is not a court and generally does not determine legal ownership of copyrighted works.

Actions taken under this Policy are administrative decisions intended to manage platform content and do not determine the legal rights of any party.

Parties remain free to pursue any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

---

## 4.8 Confidentiality

Huni will handle copyright reports in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Information submitted as part of a report may be shared with the affected user or competent authorities where reasonably necessary to process the report, resolve the dispute, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights of the parties involved.

# 5. Counter-Notification

Users who believe that their content was removed or restricted in error may submit a counter-notification for review.

A counter-notification allows the affected user to explain why they believe the reported content was lawfully posted or why the copyright report should not result in removal or restriction.

Submitting a counter-notification does not guarantee that content will be restored.

---

## 5.1 Eligibility

A counter-notification may be submitted by:

- the user who originally posted the content;
- a person authorized to act on behalf of that user; or
- another person with legal authority relating to the content.

Huni may request additional information where the authority of the submitting party is unclear.

---

## 5.2 Information to Include

To assist in reviewing a counter-notification, users should provide, where reasonably available:

- their name and contact information;
- identification of the affected content;
- an explanation of why they believe the content was removed or restricted in error;
- any information demonstrating ownership, permission, license, or other lawful basis for using the content; and
- any additional information reasonably necessary for Huni to evaluate the matter.

Incomplete submissions may require additional information before review can continue.

---

## 5.3 Review Process

Upon receiving a counter-notification, Huni may:

- review the information provided;
- request additional information from the submitting user;
- notify the reporting party where appropriate;
- seek clarification from either party;
- maintain, modify, or reverse any temporary restrictions; or
- take any other reasonable administrative action necessary to resolve the matter.

Each matter will be reviewed individually based on the available information.

---

## 5.4 Restoration of Content

If Huni reasonably determines that content was removed or restricted in error, Huni may restore access to the content.

Restoration may occur after completing any necessary review or administrative process.

Where restoration is not appropriate, the existing action may remain in effect.

---

## 5.5 Good-Faith Counter-Notifications

Counter-notifications should be submitted only where the user reasonably believes that the content was lawfully posted or that the copyright report was mistaken.

Knowingly submitting false, misleading, or fraudulent counter-notifications may violate this Policy and Huni's Terms of Service.

---

## 5.6 No Legal Determination

Huni's review of a counter-notification is an administrative process intended to manage content on the platform.

Huni does not determine the ultimate ownership of copyrighted works or resolve legal disputes between parties.

Questions concerning legal ownership remain subject to applicable law and the jurisdiction of the appropriate courts or competent authorities.

---

## 5.7 Continued Legal Rights

Nothing in this Policy prevents any party from pursuing rights or remedies available under applicable law.

Submission of a counter-notification does not prevent a copyright owner from pursuing legal action, nor does the absence of a counter-notification waive any legal rights that may otherwise exist.

---

## 5.8 Final Administrative Decision

After reviewing the available information, Huni may:

- restore the content;
- continue restricting the content;
- remove the content permanently where appropriate;
- request additional information;
- take other reasonable administrative action consistent with this Policy; or
- decline further review where sufficient information has already been considered.

Huni's administrative decision applies only to the operation of the Huni platform and does not determine the legal rights or obligations of the parties involved.

# 6. Repeat Infringers

Huni respects the rights of copyright owners while recognizing that not all copyright violations occur under the same circumstances.

Where a user repeatedly infringes the copyrights of others, Huni may take progressively stronger enforcement action to protect creators, maintain the integrity of the platform, and comply with applicable law.

Each matter will be evaluated individually.

---

## 6.1 Progressive Enforcement

When determining an appropriate response, Huni may consider factors including:

- the number of prior copyright violations;
- the seriousness of the infringement;
- whether the conduct appears intentional or accidental;
- whether the user acted in good faith;
- the user's response during the review process;
- whether the infringement was corrected voluntarily; and
- any other relevant circumstances.

Enforcement actions are intended to be fair and proportionate.

---

## 6.2 Possible Enforcement Actions

Depending on the circumstances, Huni may take one or more of the following actions:

- issue a warning;
- remove or restrict access to content;
- temporarily suspend posting privileges;
- temporarily suspend participation in certain platform features;
- suspend the user's account;
- terminate the user's account; or
- take any other reasonable action permitted under Huni's policies.

The specific action taken will depend on the facts of each case.

---

## 6.3 Serious Violations

Huni may impose immediate or enhanced enforcement where copyright infringement appears to involve:

- repeated unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material;
- commercial-scale infringement;
- intentional circumvention of copyright protections;
- fraudulent or deceptive conduct;
- coordinated infringement involving multiple accounts; or
- other particularly serious violations.

Serious violations may result in account suspension or termination without prior warning where appropriate.

---

## 6.4 Good-Faith Mistakes

Huni recognizes that copyright issues may sometimes arise from misunderstanding, uncertainty, or honest mistakes.

Where appropriate, Huni may consider evidence of good-faith conduct when determining the appropriate response.

Nothing in this section prevents Huni from taking action where necessary to protect copyright owners or comply with applicable law.

---

## 6.5 Prior Enforcement History

Huni may consider a user's prior copyright-related enforcement history when reviewing future copyright reports.

Older violations may be given less weight than more recent or repeated misconduct where appropriate.

---

## 6.6 Circumvention

Users must not attempt to circumvent copyright enforcement by:

- creating additional accounts;
- using another person's account;
- reposting content previously removed for copyright infringement;
- encouraging others to repost infringing material on their behalf; or
- using other methods intended to avoid enforcement.

Attempts to circumvent enforcement may result in additional restrictions.

---

## 6.7 Restoration of Good Standing

Where appropriate, users who demonstrate sustained compliance with this Policy may regain access to platform features following the completion of a temporary enforcement action.

The restoration of privileges is at Huni's discretion and depends on the circumstances of the case.

---

## 6.8 Consistency with Other Policies

Copyright enforcement forms part of Huni's broader content moderation framework.

Where conduct also violates the Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, or other Huni policies, Huni may apply additional enforcement measures consistent with those policies.

# 7. Removal & Restoration

Huni may remove, restrict, restore, or otherwise manage access to content as part of administering this Copyright Policy.

Actions taken under this Article are intended to protect the rights of creators, users, and the Huni community while allowing copyright concerns to be reviewed fairly.

---

## 7.1 Temporary Restriction

Where appropriate, Huni may temporarily restrict access to content while reviewing a copyright report.

Temporary restrictions may include:

- removing the content from public view;
- disabling access to the content;
- preventing further sharing;
- limiting visibility; or
- applying other reasonable administrative measures.

Temporary restrictions do not constitute a final determination that copyright infringement has occurred.

---

## 7.2 Permanent Removal

Following review, Huni may permanently remove content where it reasonably determines that:

- the content infringes copyright;
- continued availability would violate this Policy;
- removal is necessary to comply with applicable law;
- removal is required to protect the rights of others; or
- other circumstances justify permanent removal.

Permanent removal applies only to the availability of content on the Huni platform.

---

## 7.3 Restoration of Content

If Huni determines that content was removed or restricted in error, or that sufficient information supports restoration, Huni may restore access to the content.

Restoration decisions may consider:

- information provided by the reporting party;
- information provided by the affected user;
- additional supporting documentation;
- applicable law; and
- other relevant circumstances.

Restoration does not determine legal ownership of the content.

---

## 7.4 User Notification

Where reasonably practicable, Huni may notify affected users when:

- content has been removed;
- access has been restricted;
- content has been restored;
- additional information is requested; or
- other significant administrative actions are taken.

Notification may not be possible in every circumstance, including where prohibited by law or where doing so could compromise platform security or an ongoing investigation.

---

## 7.5 Preservation of Information

Where reasonably necessary, Huni may retain copies of removed or restricted content for purposes including:

- investigating copyright reports;
- resolving disputes;
- complying with legal obligations;
- responding to lawful requests from competent authorities;
- protecting platform security; or
- enforcing Huni's policies.

Retention of such information does not restore public access to the content.

---

## 7.6 Effect on Related Content

Removing or restricting content may affect related platform features, including:

- comments;
- replies;
- shared links;
- embedded previews;
- campaign submissions;
- notifications; or
- other references associated with the removed content.

Huni is not responsible for incidental changes resulting from the removal or restoration of content.

---

## 7.7 Administrative Records

Huni may maintain internal records relating to copyright reports, reviews, removals, restorations, and enforcement actions.

Such records may be used to:

- improve moderation processes;
- identify repeat infringement;
- resolve future disputes;
- comply with legal obligations; or
- protect the integrity of the platform.

Administrative records will be handled in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

---

## 7.8 Platform Administration

Decisions regarding the removal or restoration of content are administrative decisions relating to the operation of the Huni platform.

Nothing in this Article prevents any party from pursuing rights or remedies available under applicable law regarding the underlying copyright dispute.

# 8. Misuse of Copyright Claims

Huni expects all participants in the copyright reporting process to act honestly, responsibly, and in good faith.

The copyright reporting system exists to protect legitimate intellectual property rights and must not be used to harass others, suppress lawful content, interfere with legitimate community participation, or obtain unfair advantages.

Huni may take appropriate action where it reasonably determines that the reporting process has been abused.

---

## 8.1 Good-Faith Participation

Copyright reports and counter-notifications should be submitted only where the submitting party reasonably believes that their position is supported by the available facts and applicable law.

All parties are expected to cooperate honestly throughout the review process.

---

## 8.2 False or Misleading Reports

Users must not knowingly submit copyright reports that:

- contain false or misleading information;
- misrepresent ownership of copyrighted works;
- falsely claim authority to act on behalf of a copyright owner;
- intentionally omit material information;
- target content that the reporting party knows they do not own; or
- otherwise misuse the reporting process.

Knowingly submitting false reports may violate this Policy and Huni's Terms of Service.

---

## 8.3 False Counter-Notifications

Users must not knowingly submit counter-notifications that:

- contain false information;
- misrepresent ownership or permission;
- include fabricated documentation;
- falsely deny infringement; or
- otherwise attempt to mislead Huni during the review process.

Counter-notifications should be submitted honestly and only where the user reasonably believes they are appropriate.

---

## 8.4 Abuse of the Reporting Process

The copyright reporting process must not be used to:

- harass another user;
- intimidate creators;
- suppress criticism or lawful discussion;
- interfere with legitimate competition;
- retaliate against another person;
- delay or disrupt platform activities; or
- otherwise misuse Huni's administrative processes.

Copyright reports should relate only to genuine copyright concerns.

---

## 8.5 Fraudulent Documentation

Users must not submit forged, altered, fabricated, or otherwise fraudulent documents in support of a copyright report or counter-notification.

Huni may disregard documentation that appears unreliable or requires additional verification before it can be reasonably relied upon.

---

## 8.6 Administrative Action

Where Huni reasonably determines that the copyright reporting process has been abused, Huni may take appropriate administrative action, including:

- declining to process abusive reports;
- requesting additional verification;
- removing fraudulent submissions from consideration;
- issuing warnings;
- restricting access to reporting features;
- suspending accounts; or
- taking other reasonable enforcement action permitted under Huni's policies.

Enforcement will generally be proportionate to the nature and seriousness of the misuse.

---

## 8.7 Repeated Misuse

Repeated abuse of the copyright reporting system may result in progressively stronger enforcement measures.

When determining an appropriate response, Huni may consider:

- the number of prior incidents;
- whether the conduct appears intentional;
- the impact on affected users;
- cooperation during the review process; and
- any other relevant circumstances.

---

## 8.8 Preservation of Legitimate Rights

Nothing in this Article discourages users from submitting legitimate copyright reports or legitimate counter-notifications.

Huni encourages copyright owners and users alike to make good-faith use of the procedures established in this Policy.

Administrative action against misuse is intended to protect the integrity of the reporting process, not to discourage legitimate claims.
