# Plain Language Summary

Huni exists to help communities connect, share ideas, ask questions, solve problems, and build stronger local relationships.

These Community Guidelines explain what kind of behavior helps make Huni welcoming for everyone—and what behavior isn't acceptable.

In short:

• Be respectful.
• Be honest.
• Protect other people's privacy.
• Debate ideas without attacking people.
• Help make your community better.
• Report harmful behavior instead of escalating it.

Our moderators focus on behavior, not opinions.

People are free to disagree, criticize, debate, and discuss important issues.

However, harassment, threats, manipulation, fraud, and abuse are not acceptable regardless of viewpoint.

Our goal is simple:

**Anonymous to the community. Responsible to each other. Accountable to Huni.**

# 1. Welcome to Huni

Huni is a community platform built to help people participate in meaningful local conversations.

Whether you're asking questions, sharing updates, supporting local businesses, organizing community events, or discussing important issues, every contribution helps shape the experience for others.

These Community Guidelines explain the standards expected from everyone using Huni.

They apply to all content and interactions within the Services, including:

- posts;
- comments;
- direct interactions where applicable;
- Partner campaigns;
- reports;
- profile information;
- media uploads; and
- other community activities.

These Guidelines work together with Huni's Terms of Service.

Where the Terms of Service and these Community Guidelines address the same subject, they should be interpreted together.

By participating in Huni, users help create a community built on respect, trust, accountability, and constructive discussion.

# 2. Our Community Principles

These principles guide how Huni operates and how members are expected to interact with one another.

They are intended to encourage meaningful participation while protecting the community from harmful behavior.

---

## 2.1 Community First

Huni exists to strengthen communities.

Users are encouraged to contribute in ways that:

- help others;
- share useful information;
- encourage constructive discussion;
- support local initiatives;
- celebrate community achievements; and
- improve the quality of conversations.

When making decisions, Huni seeks to balance individual expression with the well-being of the broader community.

---

## 2.2 Respect Before Agreement

Healthy communities do not require everyone to agree.

Different opinions, experiences, cultures, beliefs, and perspectives are welcome.

Respect means:

- addressing ideas rather than attacking people;
- listening before responding;
- avoiding personal insults;
- remaining civil during disagreements; and
- accepting that reasonable people may reach different conclusions.

Strong disagreement is acceptable.

Personal abuse is not.

---

## 2.3 Anonymous, Never Unaccountable

Huni allows people to participate without revealing their real-world identity to other users.

Anonymity exists to encourage honest participation—not to enable harmful behavior.

Every account remains accountable to Huni through its policies, moderation systems, and applicable law.

Users should never assume that anonymity grants permission to violate these Guidelines.

---

## 2.4 Honest Participation

Communities depend on trust.

Users should participate honestly by:

- sharing information they reasonably believe to be accurate;
- correcting mistakes when appropriate;
- avoiding deceptive behavior;
- representing experiences truthfully; and
- avoiding manipulation of discussions or community systems.

Not knowing something is acceptable.

Intentionally misleading others is not.

---

## 2.5 Transparency

Huni strives to operate transparently by:

- explaining moderation decisions where appropriate;
- providing opportunities for appeals;
- communicating important policy changes;
- maintaining publicly available community standards; and
- continually improving platform governance.

Transparency builds trust between Huni and its community.

---

## 2.6 Positive Local Impact

Huni is designed to strengthen neighborhoods, municipalities, cities, provinces, and other communities.

Users are encouraged to:

- share helpful local information;
- support community initiatives;
- encourage respectful civic participation;
- promote constructive collaboration; and
- contribute to solutions rather than unnecessary conflict.

Communities become stronger when people participate responsibly.

---

## 2.7 Freedom of Expression and Responsibility

Huni supports open discussion and the respectful exchange of ideas.

People may express opinions, question decisions, criticize policies, discuss public issues, and participate in difficult conversations.

However, freedom of expression does not include the right to:

- harass others;
- threaten violence;
- promote fraud;
- invade another person's privacy;
- encourage illegal activity; or
- otherwise violate these Community Guidelines.

Moderation focuses primarily on conduct rather than viewpoints.

---

## 2.8 Shared Responsibility

Creating a healthy community is a shared effort.

Users, Partners, moderators, and Huni Technologies all play a role in maintaining a respectful environment.

Users are encouraged to:

- model respectful behavior;
- report harmful conduct;
- welcome new members;
- participate in good faith; and
- help improve the community over time.

Together, we build the kind of community we all want to be part of.

# 3. What We Encourage

Huni thrives when people participate with honesty, curiosity, kindness, and a genuine desire to improve their communities.

Every post, comment, report, and interaction contributes to the kind of community Huni becomes.

We encourage users to participate in ways that inform, support, and strengthen the people around them.

---

## 3.1 Share Helpful Information

Help others by sharing information that is:

- accurate to the best of your knowledge;
- timely;
- relevant to the community;
- useful; and
- presented with appropriate context.

Examples include:

- local announcements;
- traffic or road updates;
- emergency information;
- public service advisories;
- community events;
- educational resources; and
- opportunities that may benefit others.

---

## 3.2 Ask Questions

No one knows everything.

Questions help communities learn together.

Users are encouraged to:

- seek advice;
- ask for recommendations;
- request clarification;
- learn from local experience; and
- participate respectfully in discussions.

Constructive questions strengthen communities.

---

## 3.3 Help Others

One of Huni's greatest strengths is neighbors helping neighbors.

Examples include:

- answering questions;
- sharing local knowledge;
- offering practical advice;
- helping newcomers;
- connecting people with resources;
- supporting local initiatives; and
- encouraging positive action.

---

## 3.4 Participate Respectfully

Healthy conversations are built on mutual respect.

Users are encouraged to:

- discuss ideas thoughtfully;
- assume good faith where reasonable;
- disagree respectfully;
- acknowledge different perspectives;
- remain civil during difficult discussions; and
- contribute to productive conversations.

---

## 3.5 Support Local Communities

Huni exists to strengthen real communities.

Users are encouraged to:

- support local businesses;
- celebrate community achievements;
- promote volunteer efforts;
- recognize local heroes;
- encourage civic participation;
- highlight positive stories; and
- share opportunities that benefit others.

Communities grow stronger when people recognize and support one another.

---

## 3.6 Protect Privacy

Respect the privacy of others.

Before sharing information, consider whether:

- the information belongs to someone else;
- the individual would reasonably expect it to remain private;
- sharing the information could cause unnecessary harm; or
- permission should be obtained first.

Respecting privacy helps maintain trust within the community.

---

## 3.7 Report Problems Responsibly

If users encounter harmful content or behavior, they are encouraged to use Huni's reporting tools rather than engaging in arguments or retaliation.

Good-faith reports help moderators keep the community safe and welcoming.

---

## 3.8 Welcome New Members

Strong communities grow by welcoming new voices.

Users are encouraged to:

- answer newcomers' questions;
- provide helpful guidance;
- encourage respectful participation;
- avoid unnecessary gatekeeping; and
- help create an environment where everyone feels comfortable contributing.

---

## 3.9 Contribute in Good Faith

Participate because you genuinely want to inform, help, learn, or contribute to the community.

Good-faith participation means avoiding behavior intended to:

- provoke unnecessary conflict;
- manipulate discussions;
- spread confusion;
- waste community resources; or
- undermine meaningful conversation.

The best communities are built by people who care about improving them.

# 4. What Isn't Allowed

To maintain a safe, respectful, and trustworthy community, certain behaviors are not permitted on Huni.

These rules apply regardless of the topic being discussed or the opinions being expressed.

Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, suspension, or permanent removal from the Services.

---

## 4.1 Harassment and Bullying

Users may not engage in behavior intended to intimidate, humiliate, threaten, or repeatedly target another person.

Examples include:

- personal attacks;
- repeated unwanted contact;
- targeted harassment;
- coordinated harassment;
- encouraging others to harass someone;
- sustained insults directed at an individual; or
- mocking someone for the purpose of intimidation.

Criticizing ideas is acceptable.

Attacking people is not.

---

## 4.2 Hate Speech and Discrimination

Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups based on protected characteristics is not permitted.

Protected characteristics may include:

- race;
- ethnicity;
- nationality;
- religion;
- disability;
- age;
- sex;
- gender;
- sexual orientation;
- or other characteristics protected by applicable law.

Respectful discussion of public issues, laws, religion, politics, and social topics remains welcome.

---

## 4.3 Threats and Violence

Users may not:

- threaten physical harm;
- encourage violence;
- glorify violent acts;
- celebrate acts of terrorism;
- incite unlawful violence; or
- intimidate others through threats.

Credible threats may be referred to appropriate authorities where required by law.

---

## 4.4 Illegal Activities

Huni may not be used to:

- promote illegal activities;
- facilitate criminal conduct;
- sell or distribute illegal goods;
- organize unlawful acts;
- evade law enforcement;
- or otherwise violate applicable law.

Discussion of laws, public policy, crime prevention, or news reporting is not prohibited.

---

## 4.5 Fraud, Scams, and Deception

Users may not:

- impersonate others for deceptive purposes;
- conduct scams;
- commit fraud;
- engage in phishing;
- misrepresent identities;
- manipulate community systems;
- or intentionally deceive users for personal gain.

Honest satire, parody, or clearly labeled fictional content is generally permitted.

---

## 4.6 Spam and Platform Manipulation

Users may not:

- repeatedly post substantially identical content;
- artificially inflate engagement;
- create fake accounts to manipulate discussions;
- abuse campaigns or rewards;
- post unsolicited advertisements;
- automate abusive behavior; or
- interfere with the normal operation of the platform.

Legitimate announcements and approved Partner campaigns are not considered spam when used appropriately.

---

## 4.7 False or Misleading Information

Users should make reasonable efforts to share accurate information.

Content may violate these Guidelines if it intentionally:

- spreads false information presented as fact;
- impersonates official announcements;
- manipulates emergencies;
- encourages harmful misinformation;
- or deceives the community.

Mistakes, opinions, satire, and good-faith discussions are not automatically violations.

Moderation considers intent, context, and potential harm.

---

## 4.8 Privacy Violations

Users may not share another person's private or confidential information without appropriate authorization.

Examples include:

- home addresses;
- personal phone numbers;
- government-issued identification numbers;
- financial information;
- private communications;
- or other sensitive personal information.

Publicly available information may still violate these Guidelines if shared for harassment, intimidation, or malicious purposes.

---

## 4.9 Impersonation

Users may not falsely represent themselves as:

- another individual;
- a government official;
- a public institution;
- Huni staff;
- a Partner organization;
- or any other person or entity for deceptive purposes.

Parody and satire are generally permitted when they are unlikely to mislead others.

---

## 4.10 Harmful or Dangerous Content

Users may not post content that encourages:

- self-harm;
- suicide;
- serious physical injury;
- dangerous criminal acts;
- or other conduct likely to cause immediate harm.

Educational, medical, journalistic, or prevention-focused discussions may be permitted when presented responsibly.

---

## 4.11 Sexual Content and Exploitation

Content involving sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, or other forms of exploitation is strictly prohibited.

Such content may be removed immediately and reported to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

Other sexually explicit content may also be restricted where inconsistent with the purpose of the Services or applicable law.

---

## 4.12 Intellectual Property Violations

Users should respect the intellectual property rights of others.

Users may not knowingly:

- infringe copyrights;
- misuse trademarks;
- distribute unauthorized copies of protected works; or
- falsely claim ownership of another person's work.

Copyright concerns are addressed under Huni's Copyright Policy.

---

## 4.13 Abuse of Reporting Systems

Users should submit reports honestly and in good faith.

Users may not:

- knowingly submit false reports;
- repeatedly abuse reporting tools;
- retaliate through reporting;
- or attempt to silence others by misusing moderation systems.

False reporting may itself result in enforcement action.

---

## 4.14 Attempts to Evade Enforcement

Users may not attempt to bypass moderation actions by:

- creating replacement accounts after suspension;
- assisting permanently suspended users in evading restrictions;
- abusing technical workarounds;
- or otherwise circumventing enforcement measures.

---

## 4.15 Other Harmful Conduct

Not every harmful behavior can be anticipated in advance.

Huni reserves the right to take reasonable moderation action against conduct that:

- significantly disrupts the community;
- undermines platform integrity;
- places users at risk;
- exploits vulnerabilities;
- or is clearly inconsistent with the principles of these Community Guidelines and the Terms of Service.

This provision is intended to address unforeseen forms of abuse while remaining consistent with Huni's commitment to fair, transparent, and behavior-based moderation.

# 5. Content Standards

The quality of Huni depends on the quality of the content shared by its community.

Users are encouraged to share content that is helpful, respectful, truthful, and relevant to the communities they participate in.

The following standards apply to posts, comments, media, profile information, Partner campaigns, and other content shared through the Services.

---

## 5.1 Authenticity

Users should share content honestly and in good faith.

Content should not intentionally:

- mislead others;
- fabricate events presented as fact;
- impersonate official communications;
- manipulate community discussions; or
- falsely represent personal experiences.

Opinions, satire, parody, humor, and fictional content are welcome when they are unlikely to mislead a reasonable person.

---

## 5.2 Community Relevance

Content should contribute to the communities where it is shared.

Examples include:

- local news;
- questions;
- recommendations;
- public announcements;
- community events;
- educational discussions;
- civic participation;
- local businesses;
- public services; and
- constructive conversations.

Content that is completely unrelated, excessively repetitive, or clearly disruptive may be removed.

---

## 5.3 Original and Responsible Sharing

Whenever possible, users should share original content or content they have permission to share.

When sharing information created by others, users are encouraged to:

- provide proper attribution where appropriate;
- avoid altering information in misleading ways;
- respect copyright and intellectual property rights; and
- verify information before reposting.

---

## 5.4 AI-Generated Content

Huni welcomes responsible use of artificial intelligence and other content creation tools.

Users remain responsible for any content they publish, regardless of how it was created.

Users should not use AI or automated tools to:

- deceive others;
- impersonate individuals;
- spread misinformation;
- manipulate discussions;
- generate spam; or
- evade moderation.

Where AI-generated content could reasonably be mistaken for authentic news, official announcements, or factual reporting, users are encouraged to clearly identify it as AI-assisted or AI-generated.

---

## 5.5 Images, Videos, and Media

Users may share images, videos, audio, and other media provided they:

- have the right to share the material;
- respect the privacy of others;
- comply with applicable law;
- do not contain prohibited content; and
- are relevant to the discussion or community.

Media that contains graphic violence, exploitation, or other prohibited material may be removed.

---

## 5.6 Sensitive Content

Some discussions involve topics that may be disturbing or emotionally difficult.

Examples include:

- accidents;
- natural disasters;
- public emergencies;
- medical situations;
- crime reports; or
- other sensitive events.

Where appropriate, users are encouraged to provide context or warnings before sharing particularly sensitive material.

---

## 5.7 Commercial Content

Huni supports responsible participation by local businesses, organizations, and approved Partners.

Commercial content should:

- provide genuine value;
- accurately describe products or services;
- avoid deceptive marketing;
- comply with applicable laws; and
- respect community discussions.

Repeated unsolicited promotions, misleading advertisements, or abusive marketing practices may be treated as spam.

---

## 5.8 Public Officials and Public Interest

Discussion involving government agencies, elected officials, public institutions, businesses, and other matters of public interest is welcome.

Users may:

- ask questions;
- express opinions;
- criticize decisions;
- debate policies;
- report community concerns; and
- discuss matters affecting the public.

These discussions must still comply with all other Community Guidelines, including rules against harassment, threats, impersonation, and knowingly false information.

---

## 5.9 Editing and Removing Content

Users may edit or delete content they have shared where those features are available.

Huni may also remove, restrict, label, or otherwise moderate content that violates these Community Guidelines, the Terms of Service, or applicable law.

Removal of content does not necessarily indicate misconduct by the user; in some cases, moderation decisions are made to protect community safety, comply with legal obligations, or preserve the integrity of the Services.

---

## 5.10 Continuous Improvement

Community expectations evolve over time.

Huni may update these Content Standards to reflect:

- new technologies;
- emerging forms of online abuse;
- legal developments;
- community feedback; and
- improvements to moderation practices.

Material updates will be reflected in revised versions of these Community Guidelines.

# 6. Reporting and Moderation

Maintaining a healthy community is a shared responsibility between users, Partners, moderators, and Huni Technologies.

Huni reviews reports and moderates content to promote respectful participation, protect users, and preserve the integrity of the Services.

Moderation decisions are based primarily on conduct and compliance with Huni's policies—not on agreement or disagreement with particular viewpoints.

---

## 6.1 Reporting Content

Users are encouraged to report content or behavior they reasonably believe violates:

- the Community Guidelines;
- the Terms of Service;
- applicable law; or
- the safety and integrity of the community.

Examples include:

- harassment;
- threats;
- impersonation;
- spam;
- fraud;
- privacy violations;
- prohibited content; or
- abuse of Huni's systems.

Submitting a report does not automatically result in enforcement action.

---

## 6.2 Good-Faith Reporting

Reports should be submitted honestly and in good faith.

Users should not misuse reporting tools to:

- silence opposing opinions;
- retaliate against others;
- interfere with legitimate discussions;
- harass other users; or
- manipulate moderation outcomes.

Knowingly false or abusive reports may themselves violate these Community Guidelines.

---

## 6.3 Review Process

Reports may be reviewed using a combination of:

- automated detection systems;
- trained moderators;
- internal review procedures; and
- additional context relevant to the reported content.

Moderators consider factors including:

- context;
- intent;
- severity;
- credibility;
- potential harm;
- repeated behavior; and
- applicable legal requirements.

Not every report results in enforcement.

---

## 6.4 Behavior-Based Moderation

Huni moderates behavior—not viewpoints.

Users remain free to:

- express opinions;
- criticize governments;
- criticize businesses;
- criticize public officials;
- criticize Huni itself;
- debate controversial issues; and
- respectfully disagree with one another.

Moderation focuses on conduct such as:

- harassment;
- threats;
- fraud;
- impersonation;
- privacy violations;
- spam;
- coordinated abuse; and
- other violations of these Guidelines.

A respectful opinion is not a violation simply because others disagree with it.

---

## 6.5 Automated Moderation

Huni may use automated systems to help identify:

- spam;
- fraudulent activity;
- suspicious behavior;
- duplicate content;
- abusive activity;
- technical abuse; and
- other potential policy violations.

Automated systems assist moderation but do not replace human judgment in appropriate cases.

---

## 6.6 Human Review

Where appropriate, moderators may review reported content to better understand:

- surrounding conversation;
- user history;
- context;
- intent;
- severity; and
- applicable policies.

Moderation decisions are made using reasonable judgment based on the available information.

---

## 6.7 Possible Enforcement Actions

Depending on the circumstances, Huni may:

- provide educational guidance;
- issue warnings;
- remove content;
- restrict certain features;
- temporarily suspend accounts;
- permanently suspend accounts;
- revoke Partner privileges;
- revoke Scanner privileges; or
- take other reasonable actions consistent with these Guidelines.

Enforcement actions are intended to protect the community rather than punish disagreement.

---

## 6.8 Immediate Action

Some violations present an immediate risk to users or the platform.

In appropriate circumstances, Huni may take immediate action without prior warning, including:

- removing content;
- restricting account access;
- suspending accounts;
- preserving evidence;
- notifying affected users; or
- cooperating with lawful requests from competent authorities where required by applicable law.

Examples include:

- credible threats of violence;
- child sexual exploitation;
- severe fraud;
- account compromise;
- or other situations involving significant risk.

---

## 6.9 Transparency

Where appropriate, Huni strives to:

- explain moderation decisions;
- provide reasons for enforcement actions;
- allow users to seek clarification;
- improve moderation consistency; and
- continuously refine moderation practices.

Certain details may be withheld where disclosure could:

- compromise investigations;
- expose private information;
- create security risks; or
- violate legal obligations.

---

## 6.10 Community Cooperation

Healthy communities rely on responsible participation.

Users are encouraged to:

- report genuine violations;
- avoid escalating conflicts;
- respect moderation decisions while using available appeal processes;
- help newcomers understand community expectations; and
- contribute to a respectful environment for everyone.

Community moderation works best when users participate in good faith.

# 7. Partner Communities & Campaigns

Partners play an important role in helping communities connect with local businesses, organizations, educational institutions, government agencies, and community initiatives.

Because Partner accounts may organize campaigns, distribute rewards, and appoint Scanners, they are expected to uphold higher standards of responsibility and integrity.

These Guidelines apply in addition to Huni's Terms of Service, Partner Agreement, and other applicable policies.

---

## 7.1 Responsible Participation

Partners should use Huni to create meaningful opportunities that benefit the communities they serve.

Examples include:

- community events;
- educational activities;
- volunteer initiatives;
- local promotions;
- public information campaigns;
- civic engagement;
- charitable activities; and
- other legitimate community programs.

Campaigns should provide genuine value rather than artificially increasing platform activity.

---

## 7.2 Honest Campaigns

Partners must ensure that campaigns:

- accurately describe eligibility requirements;
- clearly explain rewards or benefits;
- state important conditions where applicable;
- avoid misleading or deceptive claims;
- comply with applicable laws; and
- are conducted fairly.

Users should never be intentionally misled regarding campaign participation or rewards.

---

## 7.3 Fair Reward Distribution

Partners are responsible for distributing rewards honestly and consistently.

Partners should:

- verify participation fairly;
- honor published campaign terms;
- avoid favoritism;
- maintain reasonable records where appropriate; and
- promptly address genuine participant concerns.

Abuse of Huni's reward systems may result in suspension of Partner privileges.

---

## 7.4 Scanner Responsibilities

Partners may appoint authorized Scanners to verify participation in campaigns.

Scanners must:

- perform verification honestly;
- use their access only for authorized purposes;
- protect participant information;
- avoid fraudulent scanning;
- report suspected abuse; and
- comply with Huni's policies.

Partners remain responsible for the actions of individuals they authorize as Scanners.

---

## 7.5 Transparency

Partners should communicate clearly with participants regarding:

- campaign objectives;
- participation requirements;
- reward availability;
- deadlines;
- important updates; and
- contact information where appropriate.

Transparency helps build trust between Partners and the community.

---

## 7.6 Community Respect

Partners should respect the communities they serve.

Partners may not:

- harass users;
- retaliate against criticism;
- manipulate discussions;
- misuse moderation tools;
- create deceptive campaigns; or
- exploit users through misleading practices.

Constructive feedback from users should be treated as an opportunity for improvement.

---

## 7.7 Public Institutions

Government agencies, educational institutions, nonprofit organizations, and other public-interest organizations are encouraged to use Huni responsibly.

Official communications should be:

- accurate;
- timely;
- relevant;
- transparent; and
- consistent with applicable legal and ethical obligations.

Verification or recognition by Huni does not constitute endorsement of the content of any communication.

---

## 7.8 Suspension of Partner Privileges

Huni may suspend or revoke Partner privileges where a Partner:

- repeatedly violates Huni policies;
- conducts fraudulent campaigns;
- abuses users;
- misuses reward systems;
- repeatedly fails to fulfill campaign commitments;
- compromises platform security; or
- otherwise acts inconsistently with these Guidelines.

Loss of Partner status does not automatically terminate a user's personal Huni account unless additional policy violations exist.

---

## 7.9 Continuous Improvement

Huni will continue improving Partner tools and policies as new community needs emerge.

Partners are encouraged to provide feedback regarding:

- campaign features;
- verification processes;
- Scanner tools;
- reward systems;
- reporting procedures; and
- other improvements that strengthen community participation.

Successful partnerships are built on trust, transparency, and shared responsibility.

# 8. Enforcement

Huni Technologies enforces these Community Guidelines to protect users, preserve meaningful discussions, and maintain trust in the platform.

Enforcement decisions are intended to be fair, proportionate, and consistent with the severity and circumstances of each violation.

The purpose of enforcement is to protect the community, encourage responsible participation, and maintain the integrity of the Services—not to punish users for expressing lawful opinions or participating in respectful debate.

---

## 8.1 Principles of Enforcement

When determining an appropriate response, Huni may consider factors including:

- the nature of the violation;
- the severity of the conduct;
- the context of the incident;
- whether the violation appears intentional;
- the potential harm caused;
- previous violations;
- cooperation during investigations; and
- applicable legal obligations.

Not all violations receive the same response.

---

## 8.2 Progressive Enforcement

Where appropriate, Huni generally seeks to encourage improvement before imposing more serious restrictions.

Enforcement may include one or more of the following:

- educational guidance;
- informal reminders;
- official warnings;
- content removal;
- temporary feature restrictions;
- temporary suspension;
- Partner privilege suspension;
- Scanner privilege revocation; or
- permanent account suspension.

The sequence of enforcement may vary depending on the seriousness of the violation.

---

## 8.3 Immediate Enforcement

Certain conduct presents significant risk to users or the platform and may result in immediate action without prior warning.

Examples include:

- credible threats of violence;
- child sexual exploitation;
- serious fraud;
- account compromise;
- repeated ban evasion;
- large-scale platform abuse;
- significant privacy violations; or
- other conduct posing immediate risk.

Immediate action may include account suspension, content removal, restriction of platform access, preservation of evidence, or cooperation with competent authorities where required by applicable law.

---

## 8.4 Repeat Violations

Repeated violations may result in increasingly serious enforcement.

Users who repeatedly ignore warnings or continue violating these Community Guidelines may be subject to:

- longer suspensions;
- additional restrictions;
- permanent loss of certain platform privileges; or
- permanent account suspension.

Repeated misconduct demonstrates unwillingness to participate responsibly within the community.

---

## 8.5 Ban Evasion

Users may not attempt to avoid enforcement actions by:

- creating new accounts after suspension;
- using another person's account;
- helping suspended users regain access;
- manipulating technical systems to bypass restrictions; or
- otherwise circumventing enforcement measures.

Attempts to evade enforcement may result in additional restrictions or permanent suspension.

---

## 8.6 Partner and Scanner Privileges

Partner and Scanner privileges are granted based on trust.

Where appropriate, Huni may suspend or revoke these privileges independently of a user's personal account.

Examples include:

- campaign abuse;
- fraudulent verification;
- repeated failure to honor campaign commitments;
- misuse of Partner tools; or
- violations affecting community trust.

Removal of these privileges does not necessarily terminate the user's personal account.

---

## 8.7 Restoration of Privileges

In appropriate cases, Huni may restore suspended privileges after considering factors such as:

- successful completion of the suspension period;
- demonstrated understanding of the violation;
- improved future conduct;
- corrective actions taken by the user; and
- the overall safety of the community.

Restoration of privileges is not guaranteed and will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 8.8 Cooperation with Investigations

Users are expected to cooperate with reasonable requests made during moderation or security investigations.

Providing false information, interfering with investigations, destroying relevant evidence, or attempting to mislead moderators may result in additional enforcement action.

---

## 8.9 Legal Compliance

Where required by applicable law, court order, or lawful request from competent authorities, Huni may preserve relevant information and cooperate as legally required.

Such cooperation will be carried out in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy, applicable law, and the protection of users' rights.

---

## 8.10 Consistency and Continuous Improvement

Huni continually reviews its moderation and enforcement practices to improve:

- consistency;
- fairness;
- transparency;
- accuracy;
- responsiveness; and
- community trust.

As new forms of abuse emerge, Huni may update its enforcement practices while remaining guided by the principles set out in these Community Guidelines.

- Partner privilege suspension;
- Scanner privilege revocation; or
- permanent account suspension.

The sequence of enforcement may vary depending on the seriousness of the violation.

---

## 8.3 Immediate Enforcement

Certain conduct presents significant risk to users or the platform and may result in immediate action without prior warning.

Examples include:

- credible threats of violence;
- child sexual exploitation;
- serious fraud;
- account compromise;
- repeated ban evasion;
- large-scale platform abuse;
- significant privacy violations; or
- other conduct posing immediate risk.

Immediate action may include account suspension, content removal, restriction of platform access, preservation of evidence, or cooperation with competent authorities where required by applicable law.

---

## 8.4 Repeat Violations

Repeated violations may result in increasingly serious enforcement.

Users who repeatedly ignore warnings or continue violating these Community Guidelines may be subject to:

- longer suspensions;
- additional restrictions;
- permanent loss of certain platform privileges; or
- permanent account suspension.

Repeated misconduct demonstrates unwillingness to participate responsibly within the community.

---

## 8.5 Ban Evasion

Users may not attempt to avoid enforcement actions by:

- creating new accounts after suspension;
- using another person's account;
- helping suspended users regain access;
- manipulating technical systems to bypass restrictions; or
- otherwise circumventing enforcement measures.

Attempts to evade enforcement may result in additional restrictions or permanent suspension.

---

## 8.6 Partner and Scanner Privileges

Partner and Scanner privileges are granted based on trust.

Where appropriate, Huni may suspend or revoke these privileges independently of a user's personal account.

Examples include:

- campaign abuse;
- fraudulent verification;
- repeated failure to honor campaign commitments;
- misuse of Partner tools; or
- violations affecting community trust.

Removal of these privileges does not necessarily terminate the user's personal account.

---

## 8.7 Restoration of Privileges

In appropriate cases, Huni may restore suspended privileges after considering factors such as:

- successful completion of the suspension period;
- demonstrated understanding of the violation;
- improved future conduct;
- corrective actions taken by the user; and
- the overall safety of the community.

Restoration of privileges is not guaranteed and will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 8.8 Cooperation with Investigations

Users are expected to cooperate with reasonable requests made during moderation or security investigations.

Providing false information, interfering with investigations, destroying relevant evidence, or attempting to mislead moderators may result in additional enforcement action.

---

## 8.9 Legal Compliance

Where required by applicable law, court order, or lawful request from competent authorities, Huni may preserve relevant information and cooperate as legally required.

Such cooperation will be carried out in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy, applicable law, and the protection of users' rights.

---

## 8.10 Consistency and Continuous Improvement

Huni continually reviews its moderation and enforcement practices to improve:

- consistency;
- fairness;
- transparency;
- accuracy;
- responsiveness; and
- community trust.

As new forms of abuse emerge, Huni may update its enforcement practices while remaining guided by the principles set out in these Community Guidelines.

# 9. Appeals

Huni Technologies recognizes that moderation decisions may occasionally require further review.

Users who believe that an enforcement action was made in error may submit an appeal in accordance with these Community Guidelines.

The appeals process exists to promote fairness, consistency, transparency, and accountability while protecting the safety and integrity of the community.

---

## 9.1 Who May Appeal

Users may appeal enforcement actions affecting their account, including:

- content removal;
- feature restrictions;
- temporary suspensions;
- permanent suspensions;
- Partner privilege revocation;
- Scanner privilege revocation; or
- other significant moderation decisions.

Minor operational decisions or technical issues may not be subject to the appeals process.

---

## 9.2 Filing an Appeal

Appeals should be submitted through the methods provided by Huni.

An appeal should, where reasonably possible, include:

- the enforcement action being appealed;
- the reason the user believes the decision should be reconsidered;
- any additional context that may assist the review; and
- any relevant supporting information.

Submitting multiple appeals for the same decision without new information may result in the appeal being closed without further review.

---

## 9.3 Review Process

Appeals are reviewed using the information available at the time of review, which may include:

- the reported content;
- surrounding context;
- prior moderation records;
- information provided by the user;
- applicable Huni policies; and
- relevant legal obligations.

Where appropriate, appeals may be reviewed by personnel who were not responsible for the original decision.

---

## 9.4 Possible Outcomes

After reviewing an appeal, Huni may:

- uphold the original decision;
- modify the enforcement action;
- restore removed content;
- restore suspended features or privileges;
- reinstate an account;
- request additional information; or
- take another appropriate action consistent with Huni's policies.

Not every appeal results in a change to the original decision.

---

## 9.5 Good-Faith Appeals

Users are encouraged to submit appeals honestly and respectfully.

Appeals should not be used to:

- harass moderators;
- delay enforcement;
- submit knowingly false information;
- repeatedly relitigate previously decided matters; or
- abuse Huni's review systems.

Abuse of the appeals process may itself result in additional enforcement.

---

## 9.6 Response Time

Huni will make reasonable efforts to review appeals as promptly as practical.

Review times may vary depending on:

- the complexity of the matter;
- the volume of pending appeals;
- the availability of relevant information; and
- applicable legal obligations.

Certain matters involving immediate risks to users or public safety may receive priority.

---

## 9.7 Final Decisions

After completing the appeals process, Huni's decision will generally be considered final.

Nothing in this section limits Huni's ability to reconsider a decision where:

- significant new information becomes available;
- a clear error is identified;
- legal obligations require further review; or
- exceptional circumstances justify reconsideration.

---

## 9.8 Continuous Improvement

Appeals help Huni improve moderation quality.

Huni may use information learned through appeals to:

- improve moderator training;
- refine moderation guidelines;
- improve automated detection systems;
- increase consistency;
- strengthen transparency; and
- improve the overall user experience.

The goal of the appeals process is not only to resolve individual cases but also to improve moderation for the entire community.
