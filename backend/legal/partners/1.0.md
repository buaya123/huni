# Plain Language Summary

This Partner Agreement explains the responsibilities of organizations and individuals who use Huni's Partner features.

Partners help bring valuable opportunities to the community by creating campaigns, distributing rewards, organizing events, and engaging with users.

Being a Partner is a privilege that comes with additional responsibilities.

In short:

- Be honest about your campaigns.
- Honor the rewards you promise.
- Protect participant information.
- Manage your Scanners responsibly.
- Follow Huni's policies and applicable laws.
- Treat community members fairly and respectfully.

Huni provides the tools to help Partners connect with communities, but each Partner remains responsible for the campaigns and activities they create.

Together, we can build trusted partnerships that benefit everyone.

# 1. Introduction

This Partner Agreement ("Agreement") governs the use of Huni's Partner features by businesses, government agencies, educational institutions, nonprofit organizations, community groups, and other approved organizations ("Partners").

This Agreement supplements Huni's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Community Guidelines, and other applicable policies.

If there is any conflict between this Agreement and the Terms of Service regarding general platform use, the Terms of Service will govern unless this Agreement expressly provides otherwise for Partner-specific activities.

By applying for, accepting, or using Partner features, the Partner agrees to comply with:

- this Partner Agreement;
- Huni's Terms of Service;
- Huni's Privacy Policy;
- Huni's Community Guidelines;
- applicable laws and regulations; and
- any additional Partner requirements communicated by Huni.

Partner features may include, where available:

- creating campaigns;
- distributing community rewards;
- appointing Scanners;
- accessing Partner management tools;
- viewing campaign analytics;
- participating in community initiatives; and
- other Partner-specific features introduced by Huni.

Partner status is granted at Huni's discretion and may be approved, declined, suspended, or revoked in accordance with this Agreement.

# 2. Eligibility

Partner status is available to organizations that wish to engage with communities through Huni's Partner features.

Because Partners may organize campaigns, distribute rewards, appoint Scanners, and represent organizations within the platform, Huni applies additional eligibility requirements before granting Partner privileges.

Approval as a Partner is a privilege, not a right.

---

Organizations that may apply for Partner status include, but are not limited to:

- registered businesses;
- government agencies;
- local government units (LGUs);
- educational institutions;
- nonprofit organizations;
- charities;
- community organizations;
- religious organizations;
- event organizers; and
- other organizations approved by Huni.

Eligibility does not guarantee approval.

A Partner account must be managed by one or more authorized representatives.

The individual applying on behalf of an organization represents and warrants that they:

Verification requirements may include, where appropriate:

- contact information;

Partners are expected to remain in good standing throughout their participation in Huni.

Partners should:

Organizations interested in using Huni's Partner features must complete the application process established by Huni.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.

Partner status becomes effective only after Huni has approved the application and activated the applicable Partner features.

---

Organizations may apply for Partner status by providing information requested by Huni.

Depending on the nature of the organization and the Partner features requested, the application may include:

- contact details;
- authorized representative information;
- intended use of Partner features;
- supporting documentation;
- verification information; and

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all information provided is accurate and complete.

Approval does not necessarily activate every available Partner feature.

Partner approval is granted to the approved organization and may not be sold, assigned, transferred, or otherwise provided to another organization without Huni's prior written approval.

Approval marks the beginning of an ongoing relationship between Huni and the Partner.

# 4. Partner Responsibilities

Partners represent organizations within the Huni community.

Because users may rely on information, campaigns, and opportunities provided by Partners, they are expected to exercise a high standard of honesty, professionalism, and accountability.

Partners are responsible not only for the campaigns they create, but also for the conduct of individuals they authorize to manage Partner features.

---

## 4.1 Honest Representation

Partners must accurately represent:

- their organization;
- their identity;
- their campaigns;
- their services;
- their products where applicable; and
- their relationship with the community.

Partners must not intentionally mislead users regarding their identity, authority, or affiliations.

---

## 4.2 Responsible Use of Partner Features

Partner tools should be used solely for legitimate organizational purposes.

Partners should use Partner features to:

- inform communities;
- organize activities;
- distribute rewards;
- promote legitimate services;
- support local initiatives; and
- encourage positive community engagement.

Partner features must not be used to manipulate discussions, artificially increase engagement, or abuse Huni's systems.

---

## 4.3 Campaign Integrity

Partners are responsible for ensuring that campaigns are conducted fairly and transparently.

Partners should:

- clearly explain campaign mechanics;
- accurately describe eligibility requirements;
- honor advertised rewards;
- avoid misleading promotions;
- communicate important updates promptly; and
- resolve participant concerns in good faith.

If unforeseen circumstances require changes to a campaign, Partners should notify affected participants as soon as reasonably possible.

---

## 4.4 Responsible Communication

Partners should communicate with users respectfully and professionally.

Partners are encouraged to:

- answer questions honestly;
- respond to reasonable concerns;
- provide accurate information;
- avoid unnecessary conflict; and
- treat participants fairly regardless of differing opinions.

Constructive criticism should not be met with retaliation.

---

## 4.5 Compliance with Laws and Policies

Partners remain responsible for ensuring that their activities comply with:

- applicable laws and regulations;
- Huni's Terms of Service;
- Huni's Community Guidelines;
- this Partner Agreement;
- Huni's Privacy Policy; and
- other applicable Huni policies.

Use of Huni does not relieve Partners of their independent legal obligations.

---

## 4.6 Protection of Community Trust

Partners should conduct themselves in ways that strengthen trust within the community.

Partners should avoid conduct that:

- damages community confidence;
- creates unnecessary confusion;
- undermines campaign fairness;
- exploits users;
- misuses organizational influence; or
- harms the reputation of the Huni community.

Community trust is fundamental to successful partnerships.

---

## 4.7 Responsibility for Representatives

Partners are responsible for the actions of individuals they authorize to manage Partner features on behalf of the organization.

Partners should ensure that authorized representatives:

- understand Huni's policies;
- receive appropriate internal guidance;
- act within their authority;

- manage Partner features responsibly.

Unauthorized or negligent actions by representatives may affect the Partner's standing.

Partners remain responsible for Scanners appointed under their organization.

Partners should:

Partners are expected to cooperate with reasonable requests from Huni relating to:

Partners are encouraged to continually improve how they serve their communities.

- refining organizational practices;

Successful partnerships are built through trust, integrity, accountability, and a shared commitment to the community.

# 5. Campaigns and Rewards

Huni provides tools that allow Partners to create campaigns and engage with communities.

Partners are solely responsible for the campaigns they create, the rewards they offer, and the commitments they make to participants.

Huni provides the platform but does not sponsor, administer, guarantee, or fulfill Partner campaigns unless expressly stated otherwise.

---

## 5.1 Creating Campaigns

Partners may create campaigns using the Partner features made available by Huni.

Campaigns should:

- provide genuine value to participants;
- clearly explain how participation works;
- accurately describe rewards;
- identify any important eligibility requirements;
- include applicable deadlines where relevant; and
- comply with Huni's policies and applicable laws.

Campaigns must not intentionally mislead participants.

---

## 5.2 Reward Commitments

Partners are responsible for ensuring that any advertised rewards are:

- available;
- accurately described;
- delivered as promised;
- distributed fairly; and
- provided within a reasonable time.

Partners should not advertise rewards they are unwilling or unable to provide.

---

## 5.3 Eligibility Requirements

Partners may establish reasonable eligibility requirements for participation, provided that such requirements:

- are clearly communicated;
- are applied consistently;
- comply with applicable law;
- do not unlawfully discriminate; and
- do not conflict with Huni's policies.

Participants should be able to understand whether they qualify before joining a campaign.

---

## 5.4 Campaign Changes

If a Partner needs to modify, suspend, or cancel a campaign, the Partner should:

- notify participants where reasonably possible;
- explain material changes;
- honor commitments already made where appropriate; and
- minimize unnecessary inconvenience to participants.

Changes should not unfairly disadvantage participants who have already complied with published campaign requirements.

---

## 5.5 Campaign Integrity

Partners must conduct campaigns honestly and fairly.

Partners may not:

- manipulate participation records;
- falsify reward claims;
- create deceptive campaigns;
- intentionally exclude eligible participants without legitimate reason;
- misuse Huni's reward systems; or
- otherwise undermine participant trust.

Campaign integrity is essential to maintaining confidence in the Huni community.

---

## 5.6 Verification of Participation

Partners are responsible for verifying participation using Huni's approved tools, including authorized Scanners where applicable.

Verification should be conducted:

- honestly;
- consistently;
- without favoritism;
- in accordance with published campaign rules; and
- using only authorized Partner accounts and Scanners.

Fraudulent verification or abuse of verification systems may result in enforcement action.

---

## 5.7 Reward Fulfillment

Unless expressly stated otherwise by Huni, Partners remain solely responsible for:

- providing rewards;
- resolving reward-related issues;
- correcting fulfillment errors;
- replacing unavailable rewards where appropriate; and
- communicating with affected participants.

Huni is not responsible for supplying, funding, or guaranteeing rewards offered by Partners.

---

## 5.8 Participant Concerns

Partners should make reasonable efforts to address participant concerns relating to:

- campaign eligibility;
- reward fulfillment;
- campaign rules;
- verification decisions;
- scheduling issues; and
- other campaign-related matters.

Where appropriate, Huni may review complaints involving potential violations of this Agreement or Huni's policies.

---

## 5.9 Fraud Prevention

Partners should take reasonable steps to prevent fraud, including:

- protecting Partner accounts;
- supervising authorized Scanners;
- monitoring unusual campaign activity;
- reporting suspected abuse to Huni;
- maintaining reasonable campaign records where appropriate; and
- cooperating with investigations.

Both Huni and its Partners share an interest in protecting the integrity of the Partner ecosystem.

---

## 5.10 Huni's Role

Huni provides the technology platform used to create and manage Partner campaigns.

Except where Huni expressly states otherwise, Huni:

- does not own Partner campaigns;
- does not guarantee campaign outcomes;
- does not guarantee reward availability;
- does not guarantee Partner performance;
- does not supervise every campaign;
- does not act as an agent of the Partner; and
- is not a party to transactions or agreements entered into between Partners and participants.

Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, agency, or franchise relationship between Huni Technologies and any Partner.

# 6. Scanner Management

Partners may authorize eligible Huni users to serve as Scanners for the purpose of verifying participation in Partner campaigns.

Scanner privileges are granted by the Partner and may be modified or revoked by the Partner or Huni in accordance with this Agreement.

Scanners act on behalf of the Partner while performing verification activities.

---

## 6.1 Appointment of Scanners

Partners may appoint one or more Scanners using Huni's Partner management tools.

Partners should appoint only individuals whom they reasonably believe to be trustworthy and capable of carrying out verification responsibilities.

Appointment as a Scanner does not create an employment, agency, or contractual relationship between Huni Technologies and the Scanner.

---

## 6.2 Nature of Scanner Privileges

Scanner privileges are limited to the verification functions made available through Huni.

Scanner status:

- does not create a separate account type;
- does not transfer ownership of Partner accounts;
- does not authorize campaign management unless separately permitted;
- may be limited to specific campaigns or organizations; and
- may be modified or revoked at any time.

Scanners retain their personal Huni accounts independent of their Scanner privileges.

---

## 6.3 Responsibilities of Scanners

Scanners are expected to:

- verify participation honestly;
- follow campaign rules established by the Partner;
- protect participant information;
- use Scanner tools only for authorized purposes;
- report suspected abuse where appropriate; and
- comply with Huni's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, and applicable Partner policies.

Scanner privileges should never be used for personal gain or unauthorized purposes.

---

## 6.4 Responsibility of Partners

Partners remain responsible for the actions of Scanners they appoint.

Partners should:

- provide appropriate instructions;
- monitor Scanner activity where appropriate;
- promptly revoke access when no longer needed;
- investigate suspected misuse; and
- cooperate with Huni regarding Scanner-related concerns.

Delegating verification responsibilities does not transfer accountability away from the Partner.

---

## 6.5 Security of Scanner Accounts

Partners and Scanners should take reasonable steps to protect Scanner access.

This includes:

- protecting account credentials;
- preventing unauthorized use;
- promptly reporting suspected account compromise;
- avoiding shared accounts; and
- maintaining appropriate device security.

Unauthorized use of Scanner privileges may result in immediate suspension of Scanner access.

---

## 6.6 Misuse of Scanner Privileges

Scanner privileges must not be used to:

- falsify participation records;
- approve fraudulent activity;
- manipulate reward distribution;
- grant unauthorized verification;
- bypass campaign requirements;
- interfere with other campaigns; or
- otherwise abuse Huni's verification systems.

Misuse may result in enforcement against the Scanner, the Partner, or both, depending on the circumstances.

---

## 6.7 Revocation of Scanner Privileges

Partners may revoke Scanner privileges at any time.

Huni may also suspend or revoke Scanner privileges where reasonably necessary to:

- protect users;
- investigate abuse;
- enforce Huni's policies;
- preserve platform security; or
- comply with applicable law.

Revocation of Scanner privileges does not automatically suspend or terminate the user's personal Huni account unless additional policy violations exist.

---

## 6.8 Audit and Investigation

To protect the integrity of Partner campaigns, Huni may review Scanner activity where reasonably necessary to:

- investigate reported concerns;
- detect fraud;
- verify compliance;
- identify unusual verification patterns;
- improve platform security; or
- enforce this Agreement.

Such reviews will be conducted in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

---

## 6.9 Future Scanner Features

Huni may introduce additional Scanner tools and capabilities over time.

Any new Scanner features remain subject to this Agreement and Huni's other applicable policies unless otherwise stated.

# 7. Data Protection & Privacy

Partners are expected to respect the privacy of Huni users and handle any information accessed through Partner features responsibly.

Access to participant information is provided solely for legitimate Partner activities and must be used in accordance with this Agreement, Huni's Privacy Policy, and applicable data protection laws.

---

## 7.1 Limited Access

Partners will only have access to information that Huni determines is reasonably necessary to support approved Partner activities.

Depending on the features used, this may include:

- usernames or assigned display names;
- participation status;
- reward eligibility;
- campaign verification records;
- Scanner activity; and
- other information made available through Partner tools.

Partners should not expect access to personal information beyond what is necessary for their legitimate activities.

---

## 7.2 Permitted Use

Information obtained through Huni may only be used for purposes directly related to the Partner's approved activities.

Examples include:

- administering campaigns;
- verifying participation;
- distributing rewards;
- responding to participant inquiries;
- resolving campaign disputes; and
- complying with applicable legal obligations.

Partners must not use participant information for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization.

---

## 7.3 Prohibited Uses

Unless expressly permitted by law or authorized by Huni, Partners must not:

- sell participant information;
- share participant information with unauthorized persons;
- collect unnecessary personal information;
- use participant information for unrelated marketing;
- attempt to identify anonymous users beyond information provided by Huni;
- misuse campaign records; or
- otherwise use participant information in a manner inconsistent with this Agreement or applicable law.

---

## 7.4 Data Security

Partners are responsible for taking reasonable measures to protect information accessed through Huni.

Reasonable measures may include:

- restricting access to authorized personnel;
- protecting account credentials;
- using secure devices where practical;
- reporting suspected unauthorized access; and
- promptly revoking access for individuals who no longer require it.

Partners are responsible for safeguarding any information they access through Partner features.

---

## 7.5 Confidentiality

Information accessed through Partner features should be treated as confidential unless disclosure is:

- authorized by the participant;
- required by law;
- necessary to administer the campaign;
- permitted under Huni's policies; or
- otherwise authorized by Huni.

Partners should take reasonable care to avoid unnecessary disclosure of participant information.

---

## 7.6 Compliance with Privacy Laws

Partners remain independently responsible for complying with all privacy and data protection laws applicable to their own activities.

Where required by law, Partners are responsible for obtaining any necessary consents, providing required notices, and fulfilling their own legal obligations.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers a Partner's legal responsibilities to Huni.

---

## 7.7 Data Incidents

Partners should promptly notify Huni if they become aware of:

- unauthorized access to Partner accounts;
- suspected misuse of participant information;
- accidental disclosure of information;
- compromised Scanner accounts;
- security incidents affecting Partner activities; or
- other events that could affect the privacy or security of Huni users.

Prompt reporting helps Huni investigate, mitigate potential harm, and protect the community.

---

## 7.8 Huni's Privacy Policy

This Article supplements, and does not replace, Huni's Privacy Policy.

Partners are expected to review and comply with Huni's Privacy Policy in addition to this Agreement.

In the event of any inconsistency regarding Huni's handling of user information, the Privacy Policy will govern.
