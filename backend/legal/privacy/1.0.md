# Plain Language Summary

This Privacy Policy explains what information Huni collects, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

Huni is built on a simple principle:

**Anonymous to the community.
Accountable to the platform.**

Your real-world identity is not displayed to other users simply because you use Huni.

However, Huni Technologies collects certain information necessary to operate the Services, protect users, prevent abuse, fulfill legal obligations, and improve the platform.

This summary is provided for convenience only.

The full Privacy Policy remains the legally governing document.
