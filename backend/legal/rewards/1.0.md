# Plain Language Summary

Huni rewards positive participation in the community.

This Rewards Policy explains how Huni Points, Experience (EXP), Levels, Badges, and Campaign Rewards work.

Our reward system is designed to encourage meaningful participation—not spam, manipulation, or unfair competition.

In short:

- Participate honestly.
- Earn rewards fairly.
- Respect campaign rules.
- Don't cheat or exploit the system.
- Rewards are benefits provided by Huni or our Partners and are not money unless expressly stated otherwise.

Huni may update the reward system over time to improve fairness, security, and community experience.

# 1. Introduction

This Rewards Policy explains how Huni's reward systems operate.

The reward system may include:

- Huni Points;
- Experience (EXP);
- Levels;
- Badges;
- Campaign rewards;
- Partner rewards; and
- future community recognition features introduced by Huni.

This Policy supplements Huni's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, and Partner Agreement.

Participation in Huni's reward system is voluntary.

By participating in campaigns or earning rewards through Huni, users agree to this Rewards Policy.

# 2. Reward Principles

Huni's reward system exists to recognize positive participation and strengthen local communities.

Rewards are intended to encourage meaningful engagement rather than competition for its own sake.

The reward system is guided by the following principles.

---

## 2.1 Fairness

Rewards should be earned through genuine participation.

Users should have a reasonable opportunity to earn rewards without unfair advantage.

---

## 2.2 Community First

Rewards exist to support healthy communities.

Activities that benefit local discussions, events, education, volunteering, and civic participation are encouraged.

---

## 2.3 Integrity

Users should earn rewards honestly.

Cheating, manipulation, automation, or fraudulent participation undermine the community and are not permitted.

---

## 2.4 Transparency

Where practical, Huni and Partners should clearly explain:

- how rewards are earned;
- eligibility requirements;
- campaign rules;
- redemption procedures; and
- any important limitations.

---

## 2.5 Continuous Improvement

The reward system will continue to evolve.

Huni may introduce new reward types, adjust earning methods, improve balancing, or retire features to maintain a healthy community experience.

# 3. Huni Points

Huni Points are digital reward points that recognize positive participation within the Huni community.

Points may be earned through eligible activities, community participation, approved campaigns, and other opportunities made available by Huni or authorized Partners.

Huni Points are intended to encourage meaningful engagement and do not represent money, stored value, or any ownership interest in Huni.

---

## 3.1 Purpose of Huni Points

Huni Points are designed to:

- recognize positive community participation;
- encourage meaningful engagement;
- support local initiatives and campaigns;
- reward constructive contributions; and
- provide access to eligible community rewards where available.

Points are intended to enhance the community experience rather than create financial value.

---

## 3.2 Earning Points

Users may earn Huni Points through activities approved by Huni.

Examples may include:

- participating in Partner campaigns;
- attending community events;
- completing eligible activities;
- contributing positively to discussions;
- achieving community milestones;
- receiving recognition through Huni features; and
- other opportunities introduced by Huni.

The number of points awarded for any activity is determined by Huni or the applicable Partner, subject to Huni's policies.

Earning opportunities may vary by location, campaign, eligibility, or platform features.

---

## 3.3 Partner-Sponsored Points

Authorized Partners may distribute Huni Points through approved campaigns using Partner features provided by Huni.

Partners do not own Huni Points.

Payments made by Partners are for access to Huni's campaign services and platform features, not for the purchase or ownership of Huni Points.

All Partner-sponsored point distributions remain subject to this Policy and Huni's approval mechanisms where applicable.

---

## 3.4 No Cash Value

Unless Huni expressly states otherwise, Huni Points:

- are not legal tender;
- have no cash value;
- cannot be redeemed for money;
- cannot be withdrawn as cash;
- are not investment products;
- do not earn interest; and
- do not represent ownership of Huni or any Partner.

Participation in Huni's reward system should not be viewed as an opportunity for financial gain.

---

## 3.5 Non-Transferability

Unless Huni expressly introduces a feature allowing otherwise, Huni Points:

- cannot be sold;
- cannot be traded;
- cannot be transferred between users;
- cannot be pledged as security;
- cannot be inherited; and
- cannot be exchanged outside Huni.

Attempting to transfer or sell Huni Points may result in enforcement action.

---

## 3.6 Point Balances

Users may view their current Huni Point balance through features made available within the platform.

Displayed balances may occasionally require adjustment to correct:

- technical errors;
- duplicate rewards;
- campaign corrections;
- fraud investigations;
- reversed transactions; or
- other legitimate administrative reasons.

Huni will make reasonable efforts to maintain accurate balances.

---

## 3.7 No Guaranteed Availability

Huni does not guarantee that opportunities to earn Huni Points will always be available.

Campaigns, activities, earning methods, and reward opportunities may change over time.

The absence of earning opportunities does not entitle any user to compensation.

---

## 3.8 Platform Integrity

Huni may limit, suspend, remove, or adjust Huni Points where reasonably necessary to:

- protect the community;
- prevent abuse;
- investigate fraud;
- enforce this Policy;
- comply with legal obligations; or
- correct administrative or technical errors.

Where appropriate, Huni may notify affected users of significant adjustments.

---

## 3.9 Future Features

Huni may introduce additional functionality involving Huni Points, including:

- redemption opportunities;
- seasonal events;
- loyalty programs;
- community recognition systems;
- charitable initiatives;
- Partner integrations; and
- other reward-related features.

# 8. Fraud & Abuse

Huni is committed to maintaining a fair, trustworthy, and community-focused reward system.

Users, Partners, and Scanners are expected to participate honestly and must not engage in activities that manipulate, exploit, or undermine Huni's reward systems.

Where reasonably necessary, Huni may investigate suspicious activity and take appropriate action to protect the integrity of the platform.

---

## 8.1 Honest Participation

Rewards should be earned through genuine participation.

Users must not attempt to obtain Huni Points, EXP, Badges, Achievements, or other rewards through dishonest or misleading conduct.

Participation should reflect legitimate community involvement.

---

## 8.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following activities are prohibited, including but not limited to:

- creating or using multiple accounts to gain rewards;
- impersonating another person or organization;
- submitting false or misleading information;
- falsifying campaign participation;
- abusing Scanner privileges;
- manipulating campaign mechanics;
- coordinating fraudulent participation with others;
- exploiting software bugs or technical errors;
- using bots, scripts, automation, or unauthorized software;
- creating fake engagements or artificial activity;
- redeeming rewards through dishonest means; or
- attempting to bypass Huni's policies or security measures.

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive.

---

## 8.3 Abuse of Campaigns

Campaigns must not be used for purposes inconsistent with their intended objectives.

Examples of campaign abuse include:

- repeatedly joining solely to farm rewards;
- organizing fraudulent participation;
- creating deceptive campaigns;
- manipulating attendance records;
- submitting duplicate participation;
- abusing referral or invitation systems; or
- interfering with the fair participation of others.

Campaigns should support genuine community engagement.

---

## 8.4 Abuse of Scanner Privileges

Scanners occupy positions of trust within Partner campaigns.

Scanner privileges must not be used to:

- approve fraudulent participation;
- verify ineligible participants;
- manipulate campaign results;
- issue unauthorized rewards;
- intentionally bypass campaign requirements; or
- otherwise compromise campaign integrity.

Partners remain responsible for the actions of Scanners they appoint, as provided in the Partner Agreement.

---

## 8.5 Exploitation of Technical Issues

Users must not knowingly exploit bugs, vulnerabilities, or unintended platform behavior to obtain rewards or advantages.

If a user discovers a technical issue that may affect the reward system, they are encouraged to report it to Huni promptly.

Responsible reporting helps improve the security and fairness of the platform.

---

## 8.6 Investigations

Huni may investigate activity that reasonably appears inconsistent with this Policy.

Investigations may include reviewing:

- campaign participation records;
- verification activity;
- Scanner actions;
- reward transactions;
- account activity;
- technical logs; or
- other information reasonably necessary to determine whether this Policy has been violated.

Investigations will be conducted in accordance with Huni's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

---

## 8.7 Enforcement

Where Huni reasonably determines that fraud or abuse has occurred, Huni may take appropriate action, including:

- removing improperly obtained rewards;
- reversing Huni Point transactions;
- adjusting EXP or user levels;
- revoking Badges or Achievements;
- canceling reward redemptions;
- restricting campaign participation;
- suspending reward privileges;
- suspending or terminating accounts; or
- taking other enforcement measures permitted under Huni's policies.

Enforcement will generally be proportionate to the nature and severity of the violation.

---

## 8.8 Cooperation

Users, Partners, and Scanners are expected to cooperate with reasonable requests during investigations relating to suspected fraud or abuse.

Failure to cooperate may be considered when determining appropriate enforcement action.

---

## 8.9 False Reports

Users should report suspected fraud only where they reasonably believe a violation has occurred.

Knowingly submitting false, malicious, or misleading reports may itself constitute abuse of Huni's systems and may result in enforcement action.

---

## 8.10 Continuous Protection

Methods used to abuse reward systems continue to evolve.

Huni may introduce additional fraud prevention measures, monitoring tools, verification methods, and security controls to protect the integrity of the platform and its communities.

Such measures will be implemented in accordance with applicable law and Huni's Privacy Policy.
